       Understanding art history from the
       perspectives of material, technique, theme
       and human interaction promises to
       integrate art theories and processual
       understanding of art.

       It elucidates how materials and techniques
       stimulate creative thinking and decision-
       making through a series of case studies,
       allowing students to explore them in their
       practice.

       The course will help students
       contextualise global connections, their
       historicity, and their impact on our lives
       and intellectual endeavours.
