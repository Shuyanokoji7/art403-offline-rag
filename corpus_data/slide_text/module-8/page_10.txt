Around 830 A.D. during the Chinese Tang Dynasty (618-907) a dhow, an Arab merchant ship, foundered on a reef just off the coast of Belitung,
a small island in the Java Sea, during its return voyage from China. A selection of the excavated cargo is exhibited at the Asian Civilization
Museum, Singapore.

“Shipwrecked: Tang Treasures and Monsoon Winds”, ACM, Singapore, 2011, Image:
