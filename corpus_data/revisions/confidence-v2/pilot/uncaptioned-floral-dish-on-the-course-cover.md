# Floral ceramic dish — course cover

Row: `m1-p01-r01` · Type: `decorative_object`

## Form & Surface

A shallow circular ceramic dish has a glossy white surface and a narrow red-edged rim. A double red circle encloses the central flower group.

## Colour & Motifs

Two upright red flowers with dark blue bases fill the centre, among green leaves and slender flowering stems. Three large blue-and-red flower groups turn around the outer field. Green leaves, looping red stems and small rosettes fill the spaces between them.

## Composition

The centre presents the flowers upright; the outer groups rotate around it. The circular frame makes the same floral vocabulary work at two scales: a small central arrangement and an enlarged surrounding repeat.

## Slide Context

Course reading: Under ‘Ebb and Flow: Art and Globalism of Oceanic Networks,’ the upper-left dish is grouped with the lower-left floral fabric (m1-p01-r02) and the trader-and-horses stone relief at right (m1-p01-r03). Flowers recur across ceramic and textile, while the relief supplies the people and traded animals that make the contact argument concrete. Together the three media introduce the circulation of goods, designs and artisanal knowledge through trade. Slides 2–3 develop that argument through the Paigah Tombs and the Coromandel kalamkari hanging, connecting ornament to human interaction and the movement of making practices.
