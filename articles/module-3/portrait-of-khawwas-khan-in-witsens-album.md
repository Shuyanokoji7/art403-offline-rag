# Portrait of Khawwas-Khan in Witsen’s album

## Composition

A half-length profile figure stands behind a low patterned parapet against a pale green ground. Narrow floral borders surround the portrait.

## Figural Style

Course reading: Turban, patterned sleeveless outer garment and a hand on a sword hilt make the military/courtly role visible.

A tiny sprig is held in the other hand.

## Text–Image Relationship

Number 42 appears above the frame. Handwritten European-language annotations sit below.

Course reading: The lecture identifies a Portuguese inscription and Witsen’s album.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

The sword descends centrally. The small flower or sprig at right balances the weapon visually.

## Slide Context

### Module 3, slide 22

**Heading context:** Mayor of Amsterdam, Nicolaas Witsen (1641–1717) and his folio of kings and royals from Deccan and Mughal kingdom

Course reading: Khawwas-Khan’s portrait is framed by Witsen’s collecting activity and Dutch commercial connections. The painted courtier, album annotation and European collector are distinct agents.
