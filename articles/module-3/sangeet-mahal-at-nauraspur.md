# Sangeet Mahal at Nauraspur

## Building Type & Function

Course reading: The named musical palace/hall at Nauraspur, introduced with Kitab-e-Nauras.

## Structural System

A broad pointed arch and narrow flanking pointed openings dominate a massive masonry façade.

## Elevation & Massing

The surviving rectangular block has a flat upper edge. Deep openings and exposed interior walls make its ruined condition visible.

## Materials & Construction Technique

Weathered stone or masonry surfaces and traces of lighter finish are visible.
