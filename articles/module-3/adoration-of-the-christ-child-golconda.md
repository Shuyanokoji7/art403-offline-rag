# Adoration of the Christ Child — Golconda

## Composition

The infant lies centrally in a cradle, surrounded by kneeling and standing figures. Airborne winged figures occupy the top. An elaborate blue-and-gold-coloured border encloses the dark floral field.

## Perspective Convention

The cradle is oblique but the figures are stacked in a shallow field rather than a unified Renaissance room.

## Figural Style

The woman beside the cradle and the infant have radiating halos. Several lower figures wear strongly patterned or contrasting local-looking clothes.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

Two hovering figures above carry objects or garlands. Another long-winged attendant stands behind the cradle. Vessels and offerings lie below. A row of small white waterbirds occupies the very bottom.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: Christian narrative appears in a Golconda painting with Persianate borders, textile-like ground and locally adapted figures.
