# Self-Portrait of Farrukh Beg — lecture identification

## External Checks

- **Qualification:** Wonder of the Age, catalogue 24, p.67, identifies a presumed self-portrait, ca.1615, in the Seitz collection. Preserve presumed: the self-identification is qualified, and publication by the Met does not mean the painting belongs to the Met. [Wonder of the Age: Master Painters of India, 1100–1900](https://resources.metmuseum.org/resources/metpublications/pdf/Wonder_of_the_Age_Master_Painters_of_India_1100_1900.pdf)

## Composition

A single elderly standing figure occupies a narrow dark garden field surrounded by nested decorated and inscribed borders.

## Figural Style

White beard, yellow turban, long ochre coat and a staff or cane emphasize age and upright bearing. One hand rises near the face.

## Text–Image Relationship

Calligraphy runs around the inner image and below it. The borders belong to an album-page presentation.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

Flowering branches frame the head and shoulders. A thin blue-white sky band appears at the top of the inner image.
