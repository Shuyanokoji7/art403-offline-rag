# House of Bijapur

## Composition

A large central enthroned figure is surrounded by seated figures on a carpet. A separate lower strip contains attendants and a white horse. Pink hills and dark water occupy the upper landscape.

## Perspective Convention

The carpet is tilted toward the viewer while figures remain upright. Dynastic ordering takes precedence over a single optical viewpoint.

## Palette & Pigment

Pink terrain, green canopies, blue carpet and gold-coloured ornament form contrasting bands.

## Figural Style

Different turbans, robes, beards and dagger hilts distinguish the assembled courtly figures.

Course reading: The lecture discusses lineage, not a documentary group sitting.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

A green parasol stands above the central throne. Additional canopies span the background.

Course reading: A small boat-like mark appears on the distant water, whose identification as the Arabian Sea is posed as a question.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “House of Bijapur documents a single gathering attended by every ruler portrayed.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: The painting constructs a dynastic grouping across generations. Its political account of lineage does not require every ruler to have attended one simultaneous gathering.
