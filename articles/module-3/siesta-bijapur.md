# Siesta — Bijapur

## Composition

A reclining figure at lower right is surrounded by three attendants beneath branching trees. An expansive blue floral border encloses the small scene.

## Perspective Convention

The reclining body, carpet and vessels occupy a shallow tilted ground. Distant pale buildings rise above dense foliage rather than sharing one rigorous vanishing point.

## Palette & Pigment

Orange-red clothing and green foliage contrast with blue and gold-coloured framing.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

A standing attendant at left holds a large red fan or fly-whisk-like object and extends a white cloth. Cups and vessels line the foreground. Small buildings are partly hidden in the upper foliage.
