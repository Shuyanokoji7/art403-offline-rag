# Yogini with a Mynah bird

## Composition

A single woman stands amid oversized flowering plants. Rocks, trees and pale buildings rise behind her. Multiple calligraphic borders surround the image.

## Perspective Convention

High horizon, stacked terrain and enlarged foreground plants create layered rather than single-point depth.

## Figural Style

Course reading: A high hair knot, orange robe, jewellery and a bird held near the face coexist, making the lecture’s ascetic-versus-royalty question understandable.

## Text–Image Relationship

Calligraphic panels frame the scene.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

The small bird rests on her raised hand at left of her face. Pink flowers flank her body, with long curling leaves on the right.

## Slide Context

### Module 3, slide 12

**Heading context:** Yogini holding a bird: Reference to Andal?

Course reading: The Yogini with a Mynah is read through its bird, dress, jewellery, plants and the heading’s question about Andal. It follows musical courtly imagery and precedes the ascetic-and-nag sequence. The grouping probes how courtliness and ascetic identity coexist without settling the figure as one named person.
