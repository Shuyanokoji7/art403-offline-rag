# Modern paper-marbling demonstration

## Studio vs Documentary Context

Modern craft-process photograph used to explain marbling, not a photograph of a seventeenth-century workshop.

## Subject & Evidence

Hands lift a sheet from a shallow tray of blue, green and yellow swirls. Commercial bottles stand behind it. Transfer of a floating or surface-borne coloured pattern to paper is demonstrated.

## Slide Context

### Module 3, slide 13

**Heading context:** Art of Abri: Marbling technique that travelled from China, Timurid Persia to India

Course reading: The marbling demonstration is placed with Ascetic Riding a Nag, followed by another thin rider on a marbled horse. Process and finished imagery explain how abri patterns become part of a figure-ground composition. The technique comparison is firmer than assigning one exclusive spiritual or literary identity to each rider.
