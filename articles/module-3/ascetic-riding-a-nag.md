# Ascetic Riding a Nag

## Composition

An emaciated rider on an emaciated horse crosses a dense field of flowing marbled bands. Angular ribs and limbs remain legible against the swirling background.

## Perspective Convention

No conventional landscape recession. The surface pattern surrounds the figures.

## Palette & Pigment

Course reading: The caption specifies ink, opaque watercolour and gold on marbled paper.

Pale bodies contrast with black, cream, red and greenish bands.

## Line & Hand

Dark fine outlines and repeated rib lines articulate the bodies. The marbled field has a different, fluid pattern logic.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

The rider extends a thin rod toward upper left. The horse droops its head. Its vertebrae and ribs project sharply.

Course reading: The paired thin bodies connect human and animal frailty within the slide’s ascetic theme.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: The ascetic subject and Sufi interpretive vocabulary are distinct from the abri process, whose travel through China, Timurid Persia and India is proposed in the lecture.
