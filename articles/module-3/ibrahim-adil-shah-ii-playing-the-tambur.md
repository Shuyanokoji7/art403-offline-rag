# Ibrahim Adil Shah II playing the tambur

## Composition

The ruler sits in the lower painted panel with a long-necked instrument across his lap, attendants at lower left and one standing figure at right. Two small monochrome European prints are mounted above.

## Perspective Convention

The central seated figure is larger than the attendants. Patterned surfaces and shallow layered space take precedence over a unified vanishing point.

## Text–Image Relationship

A narrow calligraphic strip sits above the assembled images. A large floral border with birds surrounds the entire album page.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

The instrument’s rounded body rests near the ruler’s right knee. Blossoming trees occupy the green background. Small birds dot the outer gold-coloured border.
