# Thin rider on a marbled horse — comparison image

## Composition

An angular, thin rider sits on a left-facing horse against a mostly plain warm ground. Marbling is concentrated within the horse’s body.

## Perspective Convention

The isolated side view has no deep landscape.

## Line & Hand

Rider contours and ribs are drawn. A red bridle and rod contrast with the mottled horse.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

The rider’s raised rod, projecting ribs and bent legs parallel the adjacent composition. The surrounding field is largely unpatterned.
