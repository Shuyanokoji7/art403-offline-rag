# Well south of Takht Mahal

## Building Type & Function

Course reading: Water structure within Bidar Fort, captioned fifteenth–sixteenth century.

## Plan & Spatial Organization

A partly visible rounded basin meets a masonry wall with a tall pointed recess and smaller side openings. The structure sits below the surrounding ground level.

## Structural System

Masonry retaining walls and pointed recesses are visible.

## Materials & Construction Technique

Dark block masonry and lighter lining meet the water surface.
