# Iranian ghalamzani lidded vessel

## Technique

Course reading: The slide uses ghalamzani for Iranian engraving on copper and pairs it with the zinc-alloy inlay of the Bidriware basin.

## Form & Function

A rounded lidded vessel has a short foot and tall bud-like finial.

## Decorative Vocabulary

Dense scrolls surround figural panels on the body and lid. Highlights and dark incisions structure the surface.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Ghalamzani and kalamkari name the same dyeing process on different objects.”

**Why it is wrong:** Ghalamzani concerns working a metal surface; kalamkari concerns patterned cloth and dye processes. A related tool-word does not make the materials or procedures identical.
