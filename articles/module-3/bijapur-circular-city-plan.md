# Bijapur — circular city plan

## Building Type & Function

Course reading: City map used to discuss Deccani urban planning.

## Plan & Spatial Organization

A roughly rounded fortified perimeter encloses a smaller citadel. Roads connect several gates. The urban fabric is uneven rather than a perfect geometric circle.
