# Page from Kitab-e-Nauras

## Composition

Several lines of dark calligraphy occupy pale cartouches above a large red vase filled with curling stems. Narrow coloured rules frame the page.

## Palette & Pigment

Gold-coloured ground, red vase and black script dominate.

## Text–Image Relationship

Writing is the principal content. Floral illumination integrates the text with the lower vase rather than illustrating a narrative episode.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

Small flowers and pale birds are dispersed through the illuminated ground.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The Kitab-e-Nauras page and Sangeet Mahal are evidence only of architectural patronage.”

**Why it is wrong:** The page introduces literary and musical activity beside a building. The grouping makes patronage operate across text, music, imagery and architecture.
