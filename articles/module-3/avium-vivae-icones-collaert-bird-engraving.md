# Avium Vivae Icones — Collaert bird engraving

## Process

Course reading: The caption identifies engraving: a line-based intaglio print.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: Antwerp, about 1600, with British Museum accession Z,1.59 given by the lecture.

## Composition & Motifs

Two large parrots occupy the foreground. The left bird bends toward something held near its beak. A village and much smaller animals recede between the birds.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: A European bird print is compared with a Golconda painted parrot. The media remain distinct.
