# Sailabchi basin — Bidriware

## Technique

Course reading: The lecture contrasts zinc-alloy Bidriware and inlay with Iranian copper engraving.

Bright metallic motifs stand against a dark ground.

## Form & Function

A broad fluted basin sits above a rounded lower body. A pierced central cover with knob occupies the centre.

## Decorative Vocabulary

Pointed petal-shaped lobes radiate around the bowl. Repeated pale leaf forms continue on the lower body.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Bidri’s bright decoration is ordinary white paint applied to an engraved iron surface.”

**Why it is wrong:** Inlay inserts a contrasting material; engraving cuts the surface. They are not synonyms.
