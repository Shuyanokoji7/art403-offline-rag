# Dervish receiving guests

## Composition

The shown detail groups people beside a masonry platform beneath a fruiting tree. The crop omits the full composition.

## Perspective Convention

Tree, platform and figures overlap in a compressed shallow field.

## Figural Style

A bearded figure in pale clothing stands beside a blue-toned ascetic with a yellow lower garment.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

A white bird perches high on the wall. Hand-shaped and round calligraphic standards rise near the tree. A small golden vessel sits on the masonry ledge.
