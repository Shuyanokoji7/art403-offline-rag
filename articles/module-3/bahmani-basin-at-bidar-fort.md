# Bahmani basin at Bidar Fort

## Building Type & Function

Course reading: Water basin in a palace and mosque setting.

## Plan & Spatial Organization

A lobed basin fills the foreground. Straight garden paths and hedges lead toward the palace ruins beyond. The mosque lies to the right.

## Structural System

The low shaped basin is distinct from the vaulted buildings around it.

## Ornament Vocabulary

Repeated projections around the basin turn a functional water edge into a geometric outline.
