## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The 1854 Hyderabad map is the city’s original foundation plan and proves four intact gardens.”

**Why it is wrong:** An 1854 map records the city at that time; it is not automatically the original foundation plan or proof of four intact gardens.
