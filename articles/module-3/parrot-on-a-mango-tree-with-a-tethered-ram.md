# Parrot on a mango tree with a tethered ram

## Composition

An oversized red-and-green parrot perches above a much smaller ram beside the tree trunk. The sparse turquoise ground isolates the forms.

## Perspective Convention

Hierarchy and juxtaposition replace naturalistic relative scale. The parrot is deliberately enormous beside the ram.

## Palette & Pigment

Bright red plumage, green wing and turquoise field contrast with the pale ram.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

Yellow fruits dot the mango foliage. The parrot raises a dark claw toward its beak. A red tether runs from the ram’s neck toward a peg at lower left.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The tethered ram is an incidental background detail with no compositional role.”

**Why it is wrong:** The ram anchors the lower part of the image while the larger parrot occupies the tree above. Their unequal scales and vertical placement organize the composition.
