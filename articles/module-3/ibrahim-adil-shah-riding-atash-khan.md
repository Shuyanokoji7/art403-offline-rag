# Ibrahim Adil Shah riding Atash Khan

## Composition

A large richly caparisoned elephant dominates the lower centre. Riders sit above, a smaller elephant overlaps at right, and dense flowering trees rise behind.

## Perspective Convention

Overlapping animals and high-set distant rocks create layered depth without a single-point architectural grid.

## Palette & Pigment

Dark elephant bodies, bright pink blossoms, green ground and gold-coloured cloth create the main contrasts.

## Figural Style

The ruler is differentiated by position, dress and elaborate elephant trappings.

Course reading: The caption names Atash Khan and Chanchal and identifies a European figure within the courtly scene.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

A deer-like animal stands in the upper-right clearing. Small birds and water-edge details appear below it. Floral branches arch over the elephant.
