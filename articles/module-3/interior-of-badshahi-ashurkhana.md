# Interior of Badshahi Ashurkhana

## Building Type & Function

Course reading: Ashurkhana interior in Hyderabad, a Shi‘i commemorative setting rather than an ordinary secular hall.

## Plan & Spatial Organization

A railing encloses the central display of standards. Suspended lamps and a chandelier hang between the foreground posts.

## Structural System

Visible posts support horizontal ceiling members. Painted or tiled pointed niches on the walls are not themselves proof of load-bearing arches.

## Ornament Vocabulary

Blue, green and gold-coloured tile fields, calligraphic ornament and pointed compartments cover the rear and side walls.

## Motifs & Locations

Hand-shaped and round standards stand at the rear.

Course reading: They belong to the ritual furnishing, not the wall’s structural system.
