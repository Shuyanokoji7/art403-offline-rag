# Uncaptioned domed complex — possible Ibrahim Rauza

## Plan & Spatial Organization

An axial path runs between hedges toward a central gateway. Two separate domed structures stand to either side behind it.

## Structural System

Pointed openings and domes indicate arcuate vocabulary. Interior construction is hidden.

## Elevation & Massing

Bulbous domes, slender corner towers and a long low arcade define the skyline.
