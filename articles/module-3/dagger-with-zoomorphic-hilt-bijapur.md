# Dagger with Zoomorphic Hilt — Bijapur

## Technique

Engraved gold-coloured surface, openwork and coloured eye insets are visible.

## Form & Function

A long curved, fluted blade meets an elaborate openwork hilt. The whole object at left is paired with a close-up at centre.

## Decorative Vocabulary

An elephant-like curling trunk occupies the left side. Interlocked animal bodies rise vertically. A bird appears at right above a long curling feathered tail.

Course reading: The lecture names yali/elephant and simurgh/phoenix as southern Indian and Persian vocabularies.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: This is an Indian dagger whose decorative creatures are read across cultural traditions. Motif origin is not identical to manufacture location.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The dagger hilt depicts one ordinary animal, so its significance is purely practical grip design.”

**Why it is wrong:** The hilt combines a curling trunk, bird-like forms, openwork and coloured eyes. Its composite imagery and worked surface give it decorative and courtly significance beyond gripping the blade.
