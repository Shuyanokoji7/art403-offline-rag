# Gol Gumbaz

## Building Type & Function

Course reading: A monumental tomb. The section diagram names Sultan Muhammad Adil Shah.

## Plan & Spatial Organization

A large square chamber is flanked by corner towers. The diagram includes a lower view of underground vaults and a separate arch-support plan.

## Structural System

Course reading: Eight intersecting arches support the dome according to the lecture and diagram: an arcuate system, not a forest of post-and-lintel supports.

## Elevation & Massing

A very large bulbous dome dominates a cubic base. Octagonal corner towers have repeated galleries and smaller domed caps.

## Ornament Vocabulary

Pointed façade arches, dome-base arcade and parapet details emphasize scale transitions.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Gol Gumbaz’s corner towers are the pendentives that span its central room.”

**Why it is wrong:** The corner towers are vertical elements outside the central volume. The diagram treats the spanning and supporting system of the dome separately from those towers.
