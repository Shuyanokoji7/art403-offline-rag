# Sultan Ali Adil Shah I

## Composition

A standing profile ruler holds a curved sheet of writing against an intense blue ground. Diagonal ornamental bands frame the upper corners.

## Perspective Convention

The ground is largely flat. Volume is concentrated in dress folds and the figure’s profile.

## Figural Style

A high patterned cap, gold-coloured shawl, white pleated robe and long patterned sash create a highly articulated court costume.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

A zoomorphic-looking dagger hilt projects at the waist. A circular pendant hangs on the chest. An inscription runs vertically at right.
