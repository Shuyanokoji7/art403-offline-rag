# Persianate cosmopolis

## Definition

Course reading: In the lecture’s use of Emma Flatt, Persianate culture involves shared language practice and political aesthetics across regions, while allowing exchange and conflict.

## Nearest Confusable Terms

Course reading: Persianate is broader than Persian nationality or manufacture in Iran. Shared practice does not imply one ethnicity, one religion or political harmony.

## Applied To

Course reading: Bijapur’s Siesta, travelling artists, Bidar’s madrasa and Deccani objects provide the module’s cases.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Persianate describes an object’s manufacture in Iran, regardless of where its makers worked.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: A Persianate object can be made in the Deccan by locally active artisans; the term is not a provenance label.
