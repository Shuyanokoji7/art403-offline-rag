# Part of the Kanaree Caves, Salsette

## Process

Course reading: The caption identifies this as an engraving.

Shaded tonal passages and colour fill the reproduced plate.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: These published views frame Indian places for distant viewers within the module’s discussion of colonial documentation.

## Composition & Motifs

A low dark rock ceiling spans the view. Carved figures frame a central doorway. An arched opening appears at right. Two small visitors stand at the foot of foreground steps.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: Indian landscape or rock-cut architecture is the subject. European artists and reproductive print conventions mediate its representation.
