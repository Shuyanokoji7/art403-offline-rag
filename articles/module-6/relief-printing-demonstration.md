# Relief-printing demonstration

## Studio vs Documentary Context

Contemporary process photograph. It illustrates a technique rather than identifying a historical artwork.

## Subject & Evidence

A hand peels paper from a small block with a bold black linear face. The lifted sheet exposes both the inked matrix and its impression beside a press roller.

## Composition

The diagonal lifted paper makes transfer and reversal visible. The adjacent slide diagram concerns lithography and must be read separately.
