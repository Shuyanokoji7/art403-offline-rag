# Shiva playing a veena — Battala

## Process

Woodcut context, with dense black linework and red-green colour additions.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The caption identifies a nineteenth-century Battala print from Calcutta.

## Composition & Motifs

A seated multi-faced Shiva holds a tall veena at left. Serpent heads rise around the faces. A horn-like object is held at right. Draped curtains frame the top and a small reclining bull occupies the bottom compartment.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: A regional devotional image uses the reproducibility of popular print. Neither religious subject nor bright colour identifies the print process by itself.

## Distinctions

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: The caption identifies Shiva, and the veena belongs to that devotional image. A represented musical action does not turn the deity into an ordinary documentary sitter.
