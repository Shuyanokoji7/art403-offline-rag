# Saint Sebastian woodblock impression

## Process

Relief woodcut. Raised areas carry ink, while cut-away areas remain unprinted. The paired matrix and impression make reversal visible.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The lecture gives Europe. Text and image share the printing surface.

## Composition & Motifs

Black lines describe the central bound saint, archers and the lower text. The composition reverses the orientation of the neighbouring matrix.

### Module 6, slide 4

Course reading: The Saint Sebastian woodblock and its impression are paired to make relief printing legible as a transfer process. Raised and cut-away areas relate to printed and unprinted areas. ## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The Saint Sebastian impression is the carved matrix rather than the transferred image.”

**Why it is wrong:** The impression is the image transferred from the matrix onto paper. The carved block is the separate object paired with it on the slide.
- **Complementary — [Saint Sebastian woodblock matrix](<saint-sebastian-woodblock-matrix.md>):** Course reading: Matrix and impression explain two stages of the same printing operation.
