# Unfinished textile woodblocks — Pedana

## Process

Printing matrices rather than paper impressions: carved wooden pieces retain raised outlines and partly drawn designs. The photograph shows several stages of preparation.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The named Pedana workshop supplies a living example of indigenous textile-printing skill. Neither date nor production volume is given.

## Composition & Motifs

Separate floral scroll blocks surround a broader central piece with bird-like forms and an unfinished drawn pattern.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: The local textile-printing tradition must be distinguished from the later arrival of particular European paper-print technologies.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The unfinished Pedana woodblock is itself a finished printed textile.”

**Why it is wrong:** A woodblock is the printing matrix, not the finished cloth or a paper print.
