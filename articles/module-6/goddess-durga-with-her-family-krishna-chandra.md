# Goddess Durga with her family — Krishna Chandra

## Process

Battala print with black linear pattern and restrained red-green colour.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: A named maker and Calcutta popular-print context connect devotional imagery with local production.

## Composition & Motifs

Multi-armed Durga stands centrally above a lion and a defeated figure. Other deities flank her in separate shallow niches, including elephant-headed Ganesha at far left. A broad semicircular ornamental arch encloses the group.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: A Bengali devotional ensemble is distributed in printed form.
