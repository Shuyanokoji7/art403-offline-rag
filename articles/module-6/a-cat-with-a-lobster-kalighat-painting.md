# A cat with a lobster — Kalighat painting

## Composition

An enlarged seated cat fills the sheet, holding a lobster across its mouth. Its tail curls along the bottom as a broad black mass.

## Palette & Pigment

Warm yellow-orange fur contrasts with a blue-green crustacean and red accents.

## Line & Hand

Bold contours and graduated orange washes model the pale body. Spots are large filled patches.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

A forehead mark and bead-like necklace give the cat human devotional attributes. Long lobster feelers and legs extend toward the left edge.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The Kalighat cat and the Battala cat were both printed from the same woodblock.”

**Why it is wrong:** The Kalighat example is painted and the Battala example is printed. A shared cat-and-lobster subject connects their imagery without giving them a common printing matrix.
