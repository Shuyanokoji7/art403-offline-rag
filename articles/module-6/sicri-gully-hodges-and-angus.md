# Sicri Gully — Hodges and Angus

## Process

Course reading: Metal engraving is captioned, translating Hodges’s painted view into fine tonal lines.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: These published views frame Indian places for distant viewers within the module’s discussion of colonial documentation.

## Composition & Motifs

Dense foreground trees and a winding path lead toward a pale distant hill. Tiny standing figures at lower left establish scale.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: Indian landscape or rock-cut architecture is the subject. European artists and reproductive print conventions mediate its representation.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The name Hodges gives him sole credit for both drawing and engraving Sicri Gully.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: The lecture credits Hodges and Angus in different stages of the image’s production. The drawing source and engraved realization must retain their separate maker roles.
