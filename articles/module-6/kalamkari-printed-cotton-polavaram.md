## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The Polavaram cotton uses the same paper-print support as the Saint Sebastian impression.”

**Why it is wrong:** The Polavaram object is printed cotton, while the Saint Sebastian impression is on paper. A shared relief-printing principle does not make their supports identical.
