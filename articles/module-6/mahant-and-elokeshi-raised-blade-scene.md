# Mahant and Elokeshi — raised blade scene

## Process

Monochrome Battala woodcut reproduced from a book. Repeated small marks texture clothing and furnishings.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The shared caption identifies the Mahant–Elokeshi episode, demonstrating popular print’s engagement with contemporary scandal as well as religion.

## Composition & Motifs

A standing man raises a curved blade over a woman who crouches beside a low platform and lifts both hands. A patterned column and overhead curtain stage the confrontation.
