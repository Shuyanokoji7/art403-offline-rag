# Mahavir — Chitrasala Studio

## Process

A coloured popular print.

Course reading: The caption names Chitrasala Studio.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The Pune studio location extends the module beyond Calcutta.

## Composition & Motifs

A richly crowned, jewelled seated figure occupies a symmetrical throne. Standing attendants hold fly-whisks and flowers. Two winged figures above pour streams from vessels. A sun-face and crescent-like emblem flank the central head. A small lion-like mark appears on the pedestal front.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: The captioned Mahavir subject is framed through a popular printed devotional language that includes winged attendants and modelled bodies.
