# Scene in Bombay — Grindlay, Reeve and Hogarth

## Process

Course reading: The caption specifies etching, aquatint and hand-colouring, with separate drawing, engraving and colouring credits.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: Multiple credited workers produce a reproducible urban view for circulation.

## Composition & Motifs

Palanquin bearers and groups of people populate a broad street in front of large masonry buildings. A resting figure and enclosed palanquin fill the lower right. A tall parasol marks the left group.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: Indian street life is shown through European printmaking and a colonial urban viewpoint.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Hogarth’s publication credit makes him the original sketcher and engraver of Scene in Bombay.”

**Why it is wrong:** The credits distinguish Grindlay’s source, Reeve’s printmaking role and Hogarth’s publishing role. Publishing an impression is different from drawing and engraving it.
