# Jazb al-qulub ila diyar al-mahbub — lithographed cover

## Process

Course reading: Lithographed book cover.

Dense curved calligraphy and ornamental borders exploit a process able to preserve handwritten forms.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The slide gives Lucknow, 1916, and a printer name transcribed as Mushi aval Kishur. That spelling is retained as a source issue.

## Composition & Motifs

A large central title field is framed by floral scrolls, with smaller horizontal text bands above and below.

Course reading: A modern Library of Congress label overlaps the lower printed area.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: A text in an Islamicate calligraphic tradition is reproduced using lithographic technology.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The lithographed cover is a manuscript original written separately for every purchaser.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: The cover is identified as lithographed: writing and ornament are reproduced through printing. It is not a separately handwritten original for each owner.
