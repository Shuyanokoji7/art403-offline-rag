# Misregistration — analytical study term

## Definition

Course reading: Misregistration is unintended misalignment between successive printed components, often visible where one colour shifts relative to another or to a key outline. This term is added from the corpus brief.

## Nearest Confusable Terms

Course reading: Hand-colouring outside a line, paper distortion, photographic blur and image-compression fringes can resemble misregistration but have different causes.

## Applied To

Course reading: The colour-print examples in Modules 6 and 7 provide contexts for asking how successive printed components align. The term is supplied as an analytical study concept by the corpus brief.
