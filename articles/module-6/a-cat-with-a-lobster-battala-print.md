# A cat with a lobster — Battala print

## Process

Battala woodcut context: relief printing replaces broad painted wash with patterned lines, stippled spots and repeated hatching. The slide shows a monochrome impression.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The caption locates the image in nineteenth-century Calcutta popular print culture.

## Composition & Motifs

The seated cat carries a lobster horizontally in its mouth. Its tail curls across the feet. The forehead mark and neck beads recur, while the body has many more small dark spots than the painted comparison.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: A locally circulating satirical subject crosses between indigenous painting and a reproducible print format.
