# European Print and Indian Bazaar

## Argument

Course reading: Printmaking changes knowledge circulation and popular culture while indigenous workshops select technologies, develop imagery and create markets. The module moves from colonial documentation toward local devotional and social imagery.

## Evidence & Artworks

Course reading: Landscape engravings, Solvyns’s people and market, Roxburgh’s plant plate, Kalighat–Battala comparisons and Calcutta/Pune studios establish different purposes and production systems.

## Course Throughline

Course reading: Imported processes do not determine subject matter or authorship. Local makers transform reproducible images into regional cultural forms.
