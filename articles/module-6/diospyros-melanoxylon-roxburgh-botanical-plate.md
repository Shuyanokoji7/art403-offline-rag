# Diospyros melanoxylon — Roxburgh botanical plate

## External Checks

- **Qualification:** RBGE distinguishes Roxburgh as commissioner from the Indian artists who made drawings. This corrects the common attribution shortcut; the individual makers of this specific printed plate remain unnamed here. [Indian Collections — Royal Botanic Garden Edinburgh](https://www.rbge.org.uk/science-and-conservation/library-and-archives/library-collections/image-collection/indian-collections/)

## Process

Printed botanical plate in Plants of the Coast of Coromandel.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The Latin name and analytic arrangement belong to a published botanical knowledge project. Roxburgh is named.

## Composition & Motifs

A branching stem with green leaves and a yellow-brown fruit fills the upper sheet. An isolated flowering stem appears at right. Dissected floral, fruit and bark-like details form a separate lower row.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: A South Asian plant enters a European-format scientific publication. Subject location and image-production labour should not be conflated.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Roxburgh’s name establishes that he personally drew, engraved and coloured the botanical plate.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: Roxburgh is associated with commissioning botanical drawings, while Indian artists contributed the images. Individual drawing, engraving and colouring credits for this plate are separate attribution questions.
