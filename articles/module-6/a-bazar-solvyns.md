# A Bazar — Solvyns

## Process

Course reading: Coloured etching, with linear shading under restrained colour.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The English label A Bazar and a secondary handwritten identification show how the scene was made legible for viewers outside the depicted market.

## Composition & Motifs

A street corridor narrows toward the centre. Seated sellers, standing buyers and large woven umbrellas fill both sides. A thatched stall dominates the right foreground and masonry houses rise behind.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: Calcutta market life is the subject. The representation uses a European print medium and viewpoint.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “A Bazar preserves the entire social reality of an Indian market without selection or framing.”

**Why it is wrong:** The print records an authored view of a market, not an exhaustive inventory of Calcutta commerce.
