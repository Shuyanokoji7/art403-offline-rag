# Relief, intaglio and planography

## Definition

Course reading: Relief prints from raised ink-bearing areas. Intaglio prints from recessed lines or areas. Planography separates image and non-image areas on a substantially flat surface, as in lithography.

## Nearest Confusable Terms

Course reading: Woodcut is relief. Engraving and etching are intaglio. Lithography is planographic. The surface carrying ink, not the subject or colour, determines the basic category.

## Applied To

Course reading: The Sebastian matrix/impression and relief demonstration illustrate raised surfaces. The colonial engravings illustrate intaglio. The stone and diagrams introduce lithography.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Lithography belongs to relief printing because its demonstration shows marks raised above the drawing surface.”

**Why it is wrong:** The stylized diagram’s protruding greasy marks do not make lithography a relief process.
