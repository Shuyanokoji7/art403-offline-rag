# Tiger — Tugra lithograph

## Process

Course reading: Lithograph, as captioned.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The captions locate these prints in Delhi/Lahore or Delhi and identify V&A examples.

## Composition & Motifs

Calligraphic strokes form the tiger’s body, legs, face and high curling tail. Small plants fill the ground and a domed building appears at upper left.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: Zoomorphic calligraphic imagery is reproduced with lithography. The depicted animal and written text are inseparable parts of the design.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Tiger (Tugra) is a natural-history engraving whose letterforms play no part in its animal outline.”

**Why it is wrong:** The tiger’s animal outline is built from calligraphic forms. Text and shape work together, rather than serving as a zoological engraving independent of writing.
