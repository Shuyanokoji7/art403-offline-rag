# Koummars — Solvyns

## Process

Course reading: Coloured etching according to the embedded label.

Incised-line printing is distinct from a woodcut.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The printed label identifies the subject categorically as Koummars. The lecture frames the series as colonial documentation of people.

## Composition & Motifs

A standing man in pale wrapped cloth occupies the foreground. Behind him, a seated worker handles clay or a vessel beneath a thatched canopy. Pots and upright poles locate a workshop.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: Indian occupational life is depicted through a European-authored etching and classification system.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Koummars is an unmediated photograph of potters rather than a constructed published image.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: Koummars is a published representation organized around an occupational subject. Its maker selects pose and arrangement; it is not a photographic capture.
