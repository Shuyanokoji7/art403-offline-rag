# A woman strikes a man with a broom

## Composition

Two large figures occupy a nearly empty pale sheet. The standing woman bends over a kneeling man and raises a broom diagonally above her head.

## Palette & Pigment

Black, white-grey and small red-yellow accents make the gesture legible.

## Line & Hand

Broad sweeping contours and economical shaded bands model bodies and sari folds without a detailed background.

## Figural Style

The woman holds the man near the head. His bent body and gathered hands contrast with her extended arm.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The broom scene represents a named historical scandal solely because it is a Kalighat painting.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: The visible subject is a woman striking a man with a broom. Kalighat’s capacity for social satire does not by itself attach this scene to the separately named Mahant–Elokeshi narrative.
