## Composition & Motifs

A dark rock-cut façade with rows of rectangular openings stands against a lighter rock face. Small figures and low structures occupy the foreground.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: Indian landscape or rock-cut architecture is the subject. European artists and reproductive print conventions mediate its representation.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The Salsette temple view is a measured architectural survey unaffected by composition.”

**Why it is wrong:** The print presents an arranged viewpoint with spatial framing and selected figures and terrain. It supports a picturesque reading rather than a claim to a complete measured plan.
