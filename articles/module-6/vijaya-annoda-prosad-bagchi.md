# Vijaya — Annoda Prosad Bagchi

## Process

Course reading: Hand-tinted chromolithograph, explicitly combining colour printing and additional manual tinting.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: Calcutta Art Studio and Annoda Prosad Bagchi are named, foregrounding local authorship and studio production.

## Composition & Motifs

A bearded ascetic figure carrying an elephant-headed child stands at left. A crowned woman occupies the centre, surrounded by women. One touches her face. A small plant pot, tray and seated woman anchor the foreground.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: A South Asian religious family narrative is represented with lithographic technology and modelled, receding space.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Bagchi’s Vijaya shows that Indian artists contributed subject matter but no agency to the print enterprise.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: Bagchi is a named artist within the image-making enterprise. Course reading: indigenous artistic and studio agency accompanies the adoption of print technology; it is not confined to supplying subjects.
