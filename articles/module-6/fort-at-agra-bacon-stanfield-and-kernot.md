# Fort at Agra — Bacon, Stanfield and Kernot

## Process

Course reading: Engraving, with fine linear shading across masonry, water and sky.

The credits distinguish sketch, drawing and engraved realization.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: Multiple credited makers demonstrate the stages through which a view became a reproducible image.

## Composition & Motifs

The fort rises at left above a river. Boats occupy the middle distance, and a small group of people sits or stands on rocks in the lower right. Wide sky and distant hills frame the building.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: An Indian site is represented through a European reproductive print and picturesque landscape conventions.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Kernot made every stage of Fort at Agra, including the original sketch and intermediate drawing.”

**Why it is wrong:** The credits name Bacon for the sketch, Stanfield for the drawing and Kernot for engraving. The print therefore records a division of image-making labour.
