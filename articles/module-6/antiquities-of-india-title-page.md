## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The page names Twelve Views and dedication to the Society of Antiquaries of London, presenting Indian sites within a British antiquarian publishing frame.

## Composition & Motifs

A central stone-like tablet bears the title and dedication. Palm fronds rise behind it. Carved fragments, a circular object and vegetation cluster at the base.

## Text–Image Relationship

The title resembles an inscription on an architectural object.

Course reading: The embedded imprint reads 1799 while the lecture caption gives 1808. The article’s External Checks separates the documented title-sheet date from the lecture’s issue context.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The title page’s architectural tablet is an inscription physically found at an Indian monument.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: The tablet is a pictorial device carrying the publication’s title and dedication. Architectural fragments frame that printed title rather than locating a discovered monument inscription.
