# Luna — print inset in the Gulshan album

## Process

Course reading: The lecture identifies this as a European print.

Tiny hatching is visible.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: Its placement in a Mughal album is direct material evidence of image circulation.

## Composition & Motifs

At the upper left of the folio, a woman holds a crescent while riding through clouds in a chariot. Boats and a shore appear below. LVNA labels the figure above.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: Roman lunar subject matter and European printmaking are incorporated into a Mughal album context.

## Slide Context

### Module 5, slide 18

**Heading context:** Establishing Lineage and Connections through Art Styles

Course reading: The Gulshan sheet assembles a Luna engraving with Persian drawings of a seated man, a group of men and a lion. The whole and its components must be read together: collecting, cutting, mounting and framing create a new relationship among previously separate images. One page therefore need not have one maker, date or original medium.
