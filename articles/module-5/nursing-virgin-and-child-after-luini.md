# Nursing Virgin and Child — after Luini

## Composition

A seated mother and nursing child fill the foreground against a dark interior. A small rectangular opening at upper left reveals trees, a house and distant hills.

## Perspective Convention

Soft modelling gives faces and bodies volume. The window establishes a secondary deep landscape.

## Palette & Pigment

Red dress and blue lower drapery contrast with pale flesh and dark background.

## Figural Style

The child sits upright on the mother’s lap and reaches toward her breast. The mother’s downward gaze closes the pair into an intimate group.
