# Akbar and Adham Khan

## Composition

A palace terrace occupies the upper half. A falling inverted figure connects it to a crowded lower court. Horizontal text bands divide the image without stopping the downward narrative movement.

## Perspective Convention

Tilted balconies, stacked interiors and a steep stairway expose several spaces at once. This is narrative spatial organization, not an unsuccessful single-point perspective.

## Figural Style

Robes, turbans and contrasting coloured garments distinguish court figures. Gesture and falling bodies communicate conflict.

## Text–Image Relationship

Calligraphy runs through upper and lower registers and along the bottom margin.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

Tiny birds occupy the upper-right architectural compartment. Patterned carpets and wall tiles distinguish courtly surfaces.
