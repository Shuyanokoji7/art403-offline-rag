# The Birth of the Prophet Muhammad

## Composition

A horizontal interior scene is divided by two slender red columns. A reclining woman occupies the centre-right bed, surrounded by attendants. A seated figure at far right occupies a separate compartment.

## Perspective Convention

The room is opened laterally, with overlapping figures and tilted bed rather than a single vanishing point.

## Figural Style

Layered patterned garments, lowered heads and clustered hands focus attention on the newborn at centre-left.

## Text–Image Relationship

A line of script runs above the illustration in the historical manuscript.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

Large wings extend behind two central attendants. A small hanging lamp appears above the bed. Curtains frame the far-right space.
