# Abdullah Khan Uzbek Hawking

## Composition

A mounted figure faces a standing falconer in the lower half, while red rocks, a river and small trees occupy the expansive background. Nested floral borders enclose the scene.

## Perspective Convention

The horizon is high and the landscape stacks upward. The foreground meeting remains clearly legible within it.

## Palette & Pigment

Intense red ground and rocks contrast with a blue saddle cloth, green turf and pale violet sky.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

A small dog runs between horse and falconer. A perched hunting bird rests on the falconer’s gloved hand. A quiver projects behind him. Small flowering plants line the very bottom.
