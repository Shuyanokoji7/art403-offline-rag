# Akbar’s Church — Agra

## Building Type & Function

Course reading: Christian church, captioned Agra and associated by the lecture with Akbar’s religious interests.

## Plan & Spatial Organization

A landscaped foreground leads to the central porch.

## Structural System

Columns and a horizontal entablature give the porch a trabeated form. The rear dome adds an arcuate form.

## Elevation & Massing

The photograph shows a white colonnaded entrance under a triangular pediment, a rear dome, small tower-like projections and crosses.

## Materials & Construction Technique

White rendered surfaces conceal underlying masonry.
