# Jahangir — Hashim, shared folio

## Composition

The upper compartment contains a jewelled profile ruler behind a decorated parapet, holding a small round object. A radiating halo outlines his head against the dark field.

## Text–Image Relationship

The two paintings share one folio with narrow inscriptions and a wide floral border. Separate artist/date credits are supplied for upper and lower images.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: Mughal court portraiture and Christian subject matter are brought into one album arrangement. Common framing does not make their subjects identical.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Hashim painted both Jahangir and Jesus because the images share one folio.”

**Why it is wrong:** The common accession 07A.12 belongs to the shared folio; it does not justify merging Jahangir and Jesus into one depicted person or one artist’s work.
