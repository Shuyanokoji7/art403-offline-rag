# Camel Fight — ascribed to Bihzad

## Composition

Two camels interlock their necks on a pale rocky ground. Three handlers surround them. A text box fills the upper-right corner, and small trees punctuate the blue background.

## Perspective Convention

The animals are modelled through contours and shading, while the high ground plane exposes their entangled limbs.

## Figural Style

Handlers use ropes and raised arms to engage with the fighting animals. Distinct clothes and animal coverings add local detail.

## Text–Image Relationship

Calligraphic panels form part of the page composition.

Course reading: The lecture uses the pair to discuss lineage or silsila.
