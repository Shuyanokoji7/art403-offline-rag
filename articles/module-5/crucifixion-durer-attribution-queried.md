# Crucifixion — Dürer attribution queried

## Process

A monochrome line print with dense directional hatching.

Course reading: The slide queries Dürer’s attribution and gives 1511. Specific technique requires catalogue confirmation.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The comparison demonstrates how a European printed figure could circulate as a model in a Mughal workshop.

## Composition & Motifs

Christ occupies the upper cross. The mourning woman stands at lower left and Saint John at lower right. A skull lies at the foot. INRI appears above and a monogram at lower right.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: European Christian imagery and a European print are distinguished from the Mughal brush study made after one figure.
