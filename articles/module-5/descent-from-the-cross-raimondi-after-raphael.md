# Descent from the Cross — Raimondi after Raphael

## Process

Course reading: Engraving, an intaglio process, is specified.

Dense parallel and cross-hatched lines give tone to bodies, cloth and the receding landscape.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The print is an image after Raphael, itself a reproductive relationship before its comparison with Mughal painting.

## Composition & Motifs

Two ladders flank the cross. Workers lower Christ diagonally. Mourners gather at its base. A large tree frames the right edge and a distant town fills the horizon.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: European Christian narrative circulates through a reproducible engraved image. The Mughal adaptation uses painted colour.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Raimondi’s Descent from the Cross is a unique painted manuscript page like the Mughal adaptation.”

**Why it is wrong:** Raimondi’s work is an engraving after Raphael’s design. It is a reproducible print used in comparison with the separately painted Mughal Deposition.
