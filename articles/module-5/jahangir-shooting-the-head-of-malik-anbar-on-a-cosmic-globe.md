# Jahangir shooting the head of Malik Anbar on a cosmic globe

## External Checks

- **Verification:** The Smithsonian identifies the allegory and its bull-and-fish support. The owl study explains its hostile imagery and imagined victory over Malik Ambar. The lecture spelling Anbar is retained as an alias; it does not identify a different person. [Jahangir shooting the head of Malik Ambar — National Museum of Asian Art](https://asia.si.edu/explore-art-culture/collections/search/edanmdm%3Afsg_F1948.19a/) [The Owl and the Occult — Comparative Studies in Society and History](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/comparative-studies-in-society-and-history/article/owl-and-the-occult-popular-politics-and-social-liminality-in-early-modern-south-asia/A6FCC4AB12B8787FBCFC424F775C5E06)

## Composition

The ruler stands centrally on a globe and draws a bow toward the severed head on a tall pole at left. The globe rests above a red bull and a large fish, establishing a vertical cosmic axis.

## Perspective Convention

The rounded globe is modelled and geographically marked.

Course reading: It serves allegory rather than an optically plausible space.

Slide 20 enlarges the Indian landmass and small animals on its surface.

## Figural Style

Jahangir wears a pink patterned robe, jewelled belt and turban and stands in a firm archery pose.

Course reading: The commanding stance supports the portrait’s assertion of imperial authority.

## Text–Image Relationship

Short inscriptions are dispersed around figures and objects.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

An owl perches over the enemy’s head at upper left. A balance hangs below the bow. Winged figures carrying objects occupy the upper sky. A bird-like golden form appears to the right of the ruler. An ornate upright standard stands at lower right. The bull is supported by the fish, not the reverse.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: European-style terrestrial cartography is combined with Mughal imperial portraiture and the lecture’s Islamic cosmological fish–bull support system.
