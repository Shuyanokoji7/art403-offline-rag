# Seated man — Gulshan composite folio

## Composition

A bearded seated man turns right beneath swirling cloud lines. His robe has broad empty surfaces and delicate contours. One hand reaches toward a small plant.

## Text–Image Relationship

Course reading: This is an individual component within an assembled folio, not an independent fully captioned sheet in the lecture.

## Slide Context

### Module 5, slide 18

**Heading context:** Establishing Lineage and Connections through Art Styles

Course reading: The Gulshan sheet assembles a Luna engraving with Persian drawings of a seated man, a group of men and a lion. The whole and its components must be read together: collecting, cutting, mounting and framing create a new relationship among previously separate images. One page therefore need not have one maker, date or original medium.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The seated-man drawing is a figure printed from the same plate as Luna.”

**Why it is wrong:** The seated man is a separate drawing inset mounted into the composite sheet. Luna is a distinct printed component, so the common page does not make them one plate impression.
