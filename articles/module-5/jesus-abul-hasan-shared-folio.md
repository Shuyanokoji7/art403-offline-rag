# Jesus — Abu’l Hasan, shared folio

## Composition

The lower compartment shows a youthful haloed figure in green with lowered head, holding a tall cross diagonally beside his face.

## Text–Image Relationship

The two paintings share one folio with narrow inscriptions and a wide floral border. Separate artist/date credits are supplied for upper and lower images.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: Mughal court portraiture and Christian subject matter are brought into one album arrangement. Common framing does not make their subjects identical.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The shared folio proves that Abu’l Hasan painted the emperor as a Christian ruler.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: Jahangir and Jesus are distinct subjects with separate artist credits on a shared folio. Their mounting connects images without converting the imperial portrait into a depiction of a Christian ruler.
- **Complementary — [Jahangir — Hashim, shared folio](<jahangir-hashim-shared-folio.md>):** Course reading: Upper and lower components are physically assembled on the same folio.
