# Saint John the Evangelist after Dürer

## Composition

A single standing figure fills a narrow pale sheet, with the surrounding Crucifixion scene removed.

## Line & Hand

Light contour and sparse tonal shading replace the dense hatching of the print. Several lower folds remain faint.

## Figural Style

Curly hair, lifted gaze, clasped hands and voluminous angular drapery repeat the comparison figure’s mourning pose.

## Text–Image Relationship

Small marginal inscriptions run along the lower and right edges.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: European Christian subject matter is rendered in a Mughal brush drawing with gouache, not by reproducing the engraved matrix.
