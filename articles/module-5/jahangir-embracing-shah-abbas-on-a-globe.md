# Jahangir embracing Shah Abbas on a globe

## Composition

A taller Jahangir at right embraces a smaller Shah Abbas at left before an enormous radiant disk and crescent. Their feet rest over animals on a partial terrestrial globe.

## Perspective Convention

The mapped sphere gives geographic specificity to an impossible cosmic stage.

## Palette & Pigment

Gold-coloured radiance, turquoise sky, pink and green robes and layered floral borders heighten the ceremonial effect.

## Figural Style

Contrasting stature, turbans, jewelled dress and hand placement establish an unequal pairing despite the embrace.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

A lion lies under Jahangir, a pale lamb-like animal under Shah Abbas. Winged heads support the crescent at both sides. The enlarged detail shows a foot positioned over the Indian landmass.
