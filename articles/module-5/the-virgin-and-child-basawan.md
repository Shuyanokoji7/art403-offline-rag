# The Virgin and Child — Basawan

## Composition

A reclining nursing mother and child occupy the lower right, with a pavilion rising behind and an open garden at left. Nested floral and calligraphic borders make the image an album page.

## Perspective Convention

The carpet is tilted up. The pavilion has oblique columns and roof beams, while landscape and figures retain different spatial scales.

## Palette & Pigment

Green clothing, orange drapery, blue cushions and a gold-coloured carpet dominate.

Course reading: Opaque watercolour and gold are captioned, unlike inferred pigment names.

## Figural Style

The mother rests on cushions with one hand near her cheek. The infant twists against her breast.

## Text–Image Relationship

Calligraphic cartouches above and below are visually integrated with illumination.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

An open book rests on a red cushion at left. A pale cat runs at lower right toward a shallow dish. A gold-coloured ewer stands at lower left. Behind them are a resting deer-like animal, a round well or basin, thatched structures and a small tree.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: Christian maternal imagery is reworked in a Mughal painted album page with local furnishings and a Persianate border.
