# Virgin and Child — Ghulam

## Composition

A seated mother with bent knees supports a twisting child within a mostly empty pale field, surrounded by a blue-and-gold-coloured scrolling border.

## Line & Hand

Fine dark outlines and restrained wash define folds, face and limbs. The limited colour makes drawing particularly prominent.

## Figural Style

The mother inclines her head and wears a veil and necklaces. Her seated posture spreads the drapery across the bottom.

## Text–Image Relationship

Small inscriptions appear at lower left of the figure. The slide reports that the work was made for Prince Salim.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: A Christian maternal theme is translated into a Mughal ink, opaque-watercolour and gold album image. Printed European imagery is part of the lecture’s explanation.
