# Composite European print and Persian drawings — Gulshan album

## Composition

A calligraphic band spans the top. A small Luna print occupies upper left, a seated man the middle left, three men the right, and a reclining lion the bottom. A wide floral margin with small coloured birds surrounds them.

## Perspective Convention

Each inset has its own spatial convention. The page’s unity comes from framing and arrangement.

## Line & Hand

Dark print hatching, lightly outlined figures and the shaded lion visibly differ.

Course reading: The lecture mentions Riza Abbasi without allocating every component to a hand.

## Text–Image Relationship

The album preserves differently styled fragments and scripts rather than forcing them into one continuous narrative.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: A European print is physically combined with drawings and illuminated album framing.

## Slide Context

### Module 5, slide 18

**Heading context:** Establishing Lineage and Connections through Art Styles

Course reading: The Gulshan sheet assembles a Luna engraving with Persian drawings of a seated man, a group of men and a lion. The whole and its components must be read together: collecting, cutting, mounting and framing create a new relationship among previously separate images. One page therefore need not have one maker, date or original medium.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The Gulshan composite page was created in one medium by one artist in one sitting.”

**Why it is wrong:** One assembled folio need not have one artist or production date; dates of component images and assembly differ.
