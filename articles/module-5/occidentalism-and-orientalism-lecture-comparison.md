# Occidentalism and Orientalism — lecture comparison

## Definition

Course reading: Slide 4 asks how a Mughal engagement with western images and styles differs from Orientalism. Occidentalism here is a working label for looking toward or representing the West. The slide poses a comparison rather than defining a complete theory.

## Nearest Confusable Terms

Course reading: Orientalism can name European artistic representations of the East and, in critical theory, systems of knowledge tied to unequal power. These meanings must be separated from a simple compass-direction reversal.

## Applied To

Course reading: Akbar and Adham Khan introduces the question. Christian adaptations and European print models provide the ensuing cases.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Occidentalism reverses Orientalism with exactly equivalent political power relations.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: Do not assume Occidentalism is an exact political mirror of Orientalism, or that a borrowed European motif proves colonial domination of its Mughal user.
