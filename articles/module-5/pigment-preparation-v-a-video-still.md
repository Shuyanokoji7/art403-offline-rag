# Pigment preparation — V&A video still

## Studio vs Documentary Context

A demonstration video still with V&A branding.

## Subject & Evidence

Hands hold a dark bowl of green-blue granular material over a mortar and pestle. This makes preparation work visible within a module otherwise dominated by finished paintings.
