# Canteen with Christian imagery

## Technique

Course reading: Brass with silver inlay is specified by the caption.

Pale figures and inscriptions stand against darker metal.

## Form & Function

A flattened circular container has a short neck and curved suspension loops. The broad circular face provides a continuous decorated field.

## Decorative Vocabulary

A central enthroned mother-and-child group is surrounded by inscriptions, circular medallions and bands of small figures.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: Christian imagery appears on inlaid metalwork made in Syria or northern Iraq, rather than on a European painted panel.
