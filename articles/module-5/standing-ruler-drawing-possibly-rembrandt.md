# Standing ruler drawing — possibly Rembrandt

## Composition

A standing profile ruler faces left on an otherwise nearly blank sheet. One hand raises a small stem-like object, while the other rests near a sword hilt.

## Line & Hand

Loose brown linear strokes and light hatching give the figure volume. Folds and accessories are abbreviated compared with the dense coloured Mughal portrait.

## Figural Style

A wrapped turban, necklaces, long coat and boots preserve courtly markers while the landscape and illuminated border disappear.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: The lecture’s preceding reference to Rembrandt suggests European study of Mughal portraits.
