# The Deposition from the Cross — Mughal

## Composition

Christ’s descending body lies diagonally between two ladders on either side of the cross. Mourners cluster below, and an added celestial register fills the upper blue sky.

## Perspective Convention

Figures and ladders form a deep triangular scaffold. The background combines distant architecture and small secondary groups in a compressed landscape.

## Palette & Pigment

Blue cloud forms, green landscape and bright orange-yellow drapery replace the monochrome tonal structure of the comparison engraving.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

Winged children and trumpet-playing angels float across the top. A skull and long bones occupy the foreground. Small pale and brown animals stand at lower right. A standing winged attendant appears on the right edge.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: A European Christian composition is adapted as Mughal gouache on paper, with a floral album border and added figures.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The Mughal Deposition is a mechanical reproduction that contributes no compositional alteration.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: Derivative identifies a source relationship, not a lack of invention.
- **Derivative — [Descent from the Cross — Raimondi after Raphael](<descent-from-the-cross-raimondi-after-raphael.md>):** Course reading: The paired compositions share the cross, ladders and descending body, while the Mughal version visibly alters the setting and figures.
