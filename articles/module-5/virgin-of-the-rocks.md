# Virgin of the Rocks

## Composition

Four figures form a pyramidal group in a rocky grotto. The mother’s head crowns the group, with the two children low at either side and an angel at right.

## Perspective Convention

Pale blue distant peaks recede behind darker rock openings. Graduated light and shade give faces and limbs volume.

## Palette & Pigment

Blue drapery and distant blue landscape punctuate brown-green rock and pale flesh.

## Line & Hand

Course reading: Soft tonal transitions around faces exemplify the slide’s term sfumato.

Not every edge is equally blurred.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

The left child clasps hands near a thin cross-shaped staff. The lower-right child raises a blessing hand. Small flowering plants occupy the foreground ledge.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “A dark background alone establishes sfumato in Virgin of the Rocks.”

**Why it is wrong:** Sfumato describes soft tonal transition; it is not synonymous with single-point linear perspective or with all European painting.
