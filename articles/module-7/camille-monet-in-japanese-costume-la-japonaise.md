# Camille Monet in Japanese Costume — La Japonaise

## External Checks

- **Correction:** The holding museum dates La Japonaise to 1876, not the lecture’s 1875. It identifies oil on canvas and Camille wearing a blond wig. Retain 1875 only as the original lecture assertion. [La Japonaise — Museum of Fine Arts, Boston](https://collections.mfa.org/objects/33556/la-japonaise-camille-monet-in-japanese-costume)

## Composition

A turning woman in a long red robe fills the canvas while round fans scatter across the wall and floor. The robe’s train curves across the foreground.

## Figural Style

The woman looks back toward the viewer and raises an open fan. Her light hair contrasts with the theatrical Japanese garment.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

An embroidered warrior with sword occupies the robe’s lower front. Birds and plants cover the upper red fabric. Fans contain miniature landscapes and figures.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: A European sitter performs a fashionable imagined Japan through costume and collected objects in a European painting.
