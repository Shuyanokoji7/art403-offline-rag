# MoMA Water Lilies gallery arrangement

## Physical Arrangement

The wide painting spans the far wall above a low barrier. Three dark rounded seats occupy the open wooden floor. A seated visitor at left gives bodily scale. Ceiling tracks direct light toward the painting.

## What the Arrangement Communicates

Course reading: The uncluttered floor and seating permit prolonged viewing at a distance, while the painting’s width extends beyond a narrow frontal glance. This is an inference from the visible arrangement.

## Objects & Origins

Course reading: Monet’s Water Lilies is shown in a modern museum gallery.
