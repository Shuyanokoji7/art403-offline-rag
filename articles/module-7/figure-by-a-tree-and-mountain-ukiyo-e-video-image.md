# Figure by a tree and mountain — ukiyo-e video image

## Composition

A small seated figure rests against a sloping tree trunk in front of a large mountain. Hanging branches descend from above. The video title obscures much of the lower image.

## Palette & Pigment

Muted ochre mountain, grey-blue trunk and subdued green ground.

## Line & Hand

Fine print or brush detail is too blurred to settle the source medium. The painting/manuscript category is provisional for the uncaptioned pictorial source.
