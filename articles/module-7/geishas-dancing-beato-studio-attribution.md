# Geisha’s Dancing — Beato studio attribution

## Studio vs Documentary Context

The figures pose together among arranged furnishings.

Course reading: The lecture presents this as a studio genre scene, captions a hand-coloured albumen print and credits Beato.

## Subject & Evidence

A standing woman at left holds two open fans, one raised and one lowered. A seated woman at right plays a long-necked string instrument. Tatami-like flooring, shelves and a hanging landscape frame the interior.

## Material & Colour

Applied pink, yellow and patterned textile colour overlays a photograph. Hand-colouring is distinct from colour-film capture.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Beato’s applied colour makes Geisha’s Dancing a chromolithograph rather than a photograph.”

**Why it is wrong:** The object is a hand-coloured albumen photograph. Colour added to a photographic image is different from printing a chromolithograph from colour lithographic matrices.
