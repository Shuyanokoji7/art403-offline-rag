# Courtesan after Eisen — Van Gogh

## Composition

A copied tall figure is enclosed in a bright yellow inner rectangle. An added outer field contains water, reeds, birds and other creatures.

## Palette & Pigment

Strong yellow, red, blue and green replace the original’s more restricted tones. Conspicuous strokes animate the background.

## Line & Hand

Thick dark contours and visibly separated strokes translate the print’s linear structure into painting.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

Long-legged birds stand at left, lily pads and flowers run down the water margin, and a frog-like creature occupies the bottom centre. The dragon robe motif is retained but coarsened into painted colour.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: A Japanese courtesan image is rendered in a European artist’s painting. Copying a woodblock design does not make the new work a woodblock print.
