# View of Tokyo — nineteenth-century photograph

## Studio vs Documentary Context

An urban documentary viewpoint. Selection of viewpoint remains an act of representation.

## Subject & Evidence

An elevated view follows a broad street through low tiled roofs toward a distant horizon. Tiny pedestrians and scattered taller buildings articulate the city.

## Slide Context

### Module 7, slide 14

**Heading context:** Photography and photographers in Japan

Course reading: The Tokyo street photograph and Yoshikazu’s print of French people using a camera reverse the direction of looking. Japan appears as an urban photographic subject while Europeans become the subject of a Japanese print. Orientalism/Occidentalism is therefore discussed through representation and agency, not simply the nationality of an image’s subject.
