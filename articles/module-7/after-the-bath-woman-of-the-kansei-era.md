# After the Bath — Woman of the Kansei Era

## Process

Japanese colour-print context with outlined figures, restrained flat colours and delicate pattern.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The caption dates the print 1893 while its subject refers to an earlier era.

## Composition & Motifs

A woman in a pale patterned robe stands by an open threshold and touches a cloth to her face. A small dog with a red collar stands at lower right. A pine and railing appear behind her. Title and signature panels occupy the right edge.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: A Japanese domestic subject and Japanese print are compared with Cassatt’s European domestic scene.

## Slide Context

### Module 7, slide 10

**Heading context:** Van Gogh, the search of sunlight and “Japan” in Arles, France, 1888 (continued from slide 6)

Course reading: La Japonaise, Cassatt’s The Letter and Mizuno’s After the Bath compare costume performance, domestic pictorial design and a Japanese print. The three illustrate different forms of Japanese reception. Cassatt’s intaglio medium remains distinct from woodblock, and Mizuno’s later print cannot be her direct source.
