# Japonisme — appreciation, collecting and appropriation

## Definition

Course reading: In this module, Japonisme names European engagement with Japanese art, especially prints: collecting objects, copying images and adapting cropping, pattern, contour and spatial organization.

## Nearest Confusable Terms

Course reading: Ukiyo-e is a Japanese pictorial tradition, not the name for its European reception. Orientalism asks broader questions about representing and idealizing an imagined East. Aesthetic influence does not always entail a change of medium.

## Applied To

Course reading: Eisen–Van Gogh is a direct source comparison. Cassatt–Mizuno and Hokusai–Monet are parallel comparisons whose chronology and media must remain distinct.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Japonisme requires European artists to adopt Japanese woodblock manufacture rather than adapt pictorial devices.”

**Why it is wrong:** Van Gogh and Monet adapt Japanese pictorial resources in painting, while Cassatt uses intaglio. Japonisme concerns reception and transformation, including cases where the European artist retains a different medium.
