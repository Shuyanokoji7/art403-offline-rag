# Sunflowers — lecture example

## Composition

A frontal vase sits on a horizontal tabletop against a yellow field. Upright flowers, drooping heads and heavy seed centres form an uneven oval above it.

## Palette & Pigment

Sunflowers emphasizes yellow-orange against yellow. Almond Blossoms sets pale flowers against turquoise-blue.

## Line & Hand

Separate directional strokes and assertive outlines remain visible.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: Van Gogh’s painted botanical motifs are discussed through his engagement with Japanese pictorial design, not as Japanese-made prints.

**Heading context:** Van Gogh, the search of sunlight and “Japan” in Arles, France, 1888

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: The lecture connects colour, contour and plant forms with Van Gogh’s imagined Japan. It does not identify a single Japanese print as the direct source of this Sunflowers composition.
