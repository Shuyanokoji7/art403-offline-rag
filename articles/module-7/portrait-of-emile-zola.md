# Portrait of Émile Zola

## Composition

Zola sits at left with an open illustrated book, turned toward a desk crowded with papers and books. A Japanese-style screen stands at far left. Three reproduced pictures occupy the upper-right wall.

## Palette & Pigment

A dark suit and wall contrast with pale pages and face. The reproductions create lighter inset rectangles.

## Text–Image Relationship

Books and reproductions characterize the sitter’s intellectual and artistic associations.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

The screen includes a small bird on a flowering branch. A feather quill rises from an ornamental desk vessel. A blue booklet visibly bears Manet’s name. The wall grouping contains Olympia, a Japanese standing figure and a partly obscured monochrome group.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Zola’s portrait depicts the writer without any references to collecting or artistic association.”

**Why it is wrong:** Books, the Manet booklet, the screen and the pictures on the wall construct Zola’s artistic and intellectual associations. The enlargements make those relationships part of the portrait’s reading.
