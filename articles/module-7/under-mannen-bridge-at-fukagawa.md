# Under Mannen Bridge at Fukagawa

## External Checks

- **Correction:** The Met dates the Hokusai design to ca.1830–32 and places it in Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji; the lecture’s 1823 is inconsistent with that catalogue. This verifies the design, not ownership of the particular photographed impression. [Hokusai, Under the Mannen Bridge at Fukagawa — The Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/55289?searchField=ArtistCulture)

## Process

Course reading: Japanese colour woodblock print, with sharp outlines and graded blue water and sky.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The lecture pairs the print with Monet’s Japanese bridge. Its captioned 1823 date is a source datum requiring catalogue checking.

## Composition & Motifs

A high arched bridge spans the upper image on two tall posts. Tiny people cross its crest. Boats move underneath. Distant Fuji is framed within the bridge opening. A title block stands at upper left.

## Slide Context

### Module 7, slide 11

**Heading context:** Van Gogh, the search of sunlight and “Japan” in Arles, France, 1888 (continued from slide 6)

Course reading: Hokusai’s Mannen Bridge and Monet’s Japanese bridge are paired through the bridge’s graphic curve. The populated urban crossing and garden pond have different scales, settings and media. The comparison makes a formal relation explicit without establishing that Monet copied this exact impression.
