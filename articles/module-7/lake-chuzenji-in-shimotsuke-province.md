# Lake Chuzenji in Shimotsuke Province

## Process

Course reading: Colour woodblock-print context.

Separate colour fields, key outlines and graded blue water are visible.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: A place-specific Japanese print becomes part of the module’s account of European access to Japanese imagery.

## Composition & Motifs

Two massive foreground tree trunks cut vertically through a lake and mountain view. A torii stands near the lower-right shoreline. Small travellers descend beside a railing at right. Tiny boats and lakeside buildings appear at left. Red title labels occupy the upper right.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Lake Chuzenji and The Great Wave are two titles for the same Japanese landscape print.”

**Why it is wrong:** Lake Chuzenji is a separate landscape organized through a foreground frame of trees. The Great Wave instead juxtaposes a dominant wave, boats and distant Fuji.
