# Claude Monet — photographic portrait in video

## Studio vs Documentary Context

Course reading: The video labels the sitter and uses the photograph in an account of Japonisme.

Tape and film-strip graphics frame the portrait in the video design.

## Subject & Evidence

A bearded man in a hat stands beside a lily pond, one hand near his hip. Garden vegetation and water place the portrait outdoors.

### Module 7, slide 12

**Heading context:** Van Gogh, the search of sunlight and “Japan” in Arles, France, 1888 (continued from slide 6)
