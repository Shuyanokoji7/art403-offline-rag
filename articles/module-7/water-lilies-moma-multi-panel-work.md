# Water Lilies — MoMA multi-panel work

## Composition

A very wide, multi-panel field stretches horizontally across the photographed gallery wall. Patches of lilies and reflected foliage alternate with blue-violet open water.

## Perspective Convention

The expansive surface has no single horizon or central focal object.

## Palette & Pigment

Blue-green fields alternate with pale pink, cream and violet passages.

## Line & Hand

Broad passages and scattered marks merge at viewing distance.
