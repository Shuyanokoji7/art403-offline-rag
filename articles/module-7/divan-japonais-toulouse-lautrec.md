# Divan Japonais — Toulouse-Lautrec

## Process

Course reading: Colour lithographic poster context.

Flat colour areas and bold contour are visible.

Course reading: Stone count requires catalogue evidence.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: Large title lettering identifies entertainment and celebrity in a reproducible urban advertisement.

## Composition & Motifs

A black-clad woman in a large hat dominates the seated foreground beside a top-hatted man at right. The stage performer is cropped at upper left. Musicians and instruments fill the space between audience and stage.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: Parisian nightlife is represented in European lithography with compositional devices discussed under Japanese influence.

## Slide Context

### Module 7, slide 9

**Heading context:** Van Gogh, the search of sunlight and “Japan” in Arles, France, 1888 (continued from slide 6)

Course reading: Jane Avril and Divan Japonais place Japanese-inspired cropping and contour within Parisian entertainment publicity. Performer, spectator and instrument are organized differently in the two posters. Their subject and lithographic process remain distinct from Japanese woodblock manufacture.
