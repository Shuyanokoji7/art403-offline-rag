# Almond Blossoms

## Composition

Branches spread diagonally across an uninterrupted blue sky, cropped on every side. Pale blossoms and dark-edged twigs form an all-over surface without a horizon.

## Palette & Pigment

Sunflowers emphasizes yellow-orange against yellow. Almond Blossoms sets pale flowers against turquoise-blue.

## Line & Hand

Separate directional strokes and assertive outlines remain visible.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: Van Gogh’s painted botanical motifs are discussed through his engagement with Japanese pictorial design, not as Japanese-made prints.
