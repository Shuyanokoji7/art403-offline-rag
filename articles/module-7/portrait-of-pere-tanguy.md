# Portrait of Père Tanguy

## Composition

The seated frontal man forms a broad central triangle with folded hands. Japanese images cover the wall behind him, cropping around his shoulders and hat.

## Palette & Pigment

Blue-green coat, warm face and bright yellow-orange background contrast through separate directional strokes.

## Line & Hand

Short strokes model the face and jacket, unlike the flatter reproduced prints behind him.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

A mountain image appears above the hat. Female figures flank both shoulders and the lower-right edge. Floral imagery occupies the lower left.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: A French sitter is painted by Van Gogh among Japanese images: collecting becomes part of portraiture rather than proof the sitter is Japanese.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The Japanese images behind Père Tanguy establish that the portrait was painted in a Japanese room observed on location.”

**Why it is wrong:** The background expresses an artist’s collected and imagined Japan; it does not depict a Japanese room observed on location.
