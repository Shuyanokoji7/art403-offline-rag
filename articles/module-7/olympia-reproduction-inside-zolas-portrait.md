# Olympia reproduction inside Zola’s portrait

## Process

A represented reproduction within Manet’s painting, not a separately photographed original print.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The wall arrangement demonstrates association and collecting. Component identities beyond Olympia are not individually captioned in the lecture.

## Composition & Motifs

A reclining nude lies on white bedding in a dark horizontal rectangle at the front of the wall grouping. An attendant stands at right.

## Slide Context

### Module 7, slide 7

**Heading context:** Van Gogh, the search of sunlight and “Japan” in Arles, France, 1888 (continued from slide 6)

Course reading: Zola’s portrait and the enlarged reproductions on its wall make artistic association visible inside an interior. Olympia, a Japanese figure and a monochrome group are objects of pictorial reference within Manet’s painting. The components connect collecting to portrait identity without becoming separately inspected original prints.
