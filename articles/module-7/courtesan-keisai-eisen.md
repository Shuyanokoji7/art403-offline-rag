# Courtesan — Keisai Eisen

## Process

Course reading: Japanese colour woodblock print.

Fine dark contours enclose large patterned garment areas.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The caption identifies this as the source image compared with Van Gogh’s copy.

## Composition & Motifs

A tall turning figure fills a narrow sheet. Numerous hairpins radiate around the coiffure. A large dragon and curling waves decorate the dark robe. Signature marks lie low at left.

## Subject Matter vs Technique Origin

Course reading: The original Japanese subject and print format are transferred into a European painted adaptation.
