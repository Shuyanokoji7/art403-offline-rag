# The Ship of Tarshish — Prasad Raghavan

## Subject & Narrative

A boat-shaped sculptural hull carries a rectangular stack of small container-like boxes.

Course reading: Its title connects a contemporary cargo form with the lecture’s Biblical trade discussion.

## Iconographic Attributes

The title is written on the hull. Container forms make modern logistics visible.

## Material & Technique

Constructed installation with a dark angular hull and repeated box modules.

## Architectural Placement

The work stands inside a long rough-walled hall with repeated windows and exposed roof structure. The hull’s pointed end projects toward the viewer.
