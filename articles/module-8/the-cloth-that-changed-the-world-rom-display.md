# The Cloth that Changed the World — ROM display

## Physical Arrangement

Two mannequins stand in a large foreground glass case: a dark patterned drape at left and a yellow garment behind it. A tall textile with dark rock-like forms rises centrally. Beyond, pale and red figured hangings occupy staggered panels. A large medallion textile hangs on a purple panel at right, beside a coat with sleeves extended. Angled labels lie low along the case front, which reads India Today / L’Inde moderne.

## What the Arrangement Communicates

Course reading: Draped clothing, extended garments and flat hangings expose different textile functions. The modern-India label places living practice beside historical cloth within the photographed sightline.

## Objects & Origins

Course reading: The caption names the Royal Ontario Museum’s Indian chintz exhibition, 2020–21.
