# Oceanic history and connected art histories

## Argument

Course reading: The lecture places art history alongside histories of the Mediterranean and Indian Ocean, naming Braudel, K. N. Chaudhuri and Ashin Das Gupta. It asks how political, social and cultural networks can organize the study of art.

## Evidence & Artworks

Course reading: Trade-textile exhibitions, shipwreck cargo and contemporary installations supply cases of movement, serial production and changing object lives.

## Course Throughline

Course reading: The ocean connects production, exchange, display and interpretation. It is both a historical route and an active contemporary medium.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Connected history means every object followed one identical and harmonious trade route.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: Connected history does not mean every object has an identical route or that cultural contact was always harmonious.
