# The Cross — Subodh Kerkar

## Subject & Narrative

A cross with widening arm ends is densely encrusted with shells. Its recognizable outline survives while the surface becomes irregular and organic.

## Iconographic Attributes

The cross is a Christian form. No corpus of Christ is attached in this view.

## Material & Technique

Pale cupped shells accumulate over a dark substrate, with several larger white shells below the crossing. The slide asks about collaboration with the ocean.

## Architectural Placement

Photographed on a plain pale wall, then repeated as The Oceanic Cross in a Museum of Goa video card.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The Cross presents the ocean only as an image depicted on its surface, without material participation.”

**Why it is wrong:** Shell-like accretions cover the cross’s surface and alter its texture. Course reading: the ocean participates materially in the making discussion, while the artist selects and presents the recognizable cross form.
- **Complementary — [Belitung cargo — bowls arranged as waves around a dhow](<belitung-cargo-bowls-arranged-as-waves-around-a-dhow.md>):** Course reading: The slide connects historical maritime objects and contemporary ocean-mediated making.
