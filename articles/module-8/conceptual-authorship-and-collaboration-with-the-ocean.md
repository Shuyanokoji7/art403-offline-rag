# Conceptual authorship and collaboration with the ocean

## Definition

Course reading: An analytical term for the lecture’s question about Kerkar: artistic authorship may involve setting conditions for environmental growth or transformation, rather than personally making every visible mark. The term itself is supplied by the corpus brief. The slide asks the question of collaboration.

## Nearest Confusable Terms

Course reading: Conceptual authorship is not absence of material work. Natural transformation, workshop collaboration and viewer participation distribute agency differently and require object-specific evidence.

## Applied To

Course reading: The shell-encrusted Cross is the main case. The photograph of a visitor acting on Tallur’s log provides a distinct participatory comparison.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Calling the ocean a collaborator removes the artist’s decisions about form, selection and presentation.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: Material accretion and environmental change can participate alongside the artist’s choice of form, duration, selection and display. Collaboration distributes agency rather than erasing artistic decisions.
