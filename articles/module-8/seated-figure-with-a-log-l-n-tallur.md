# Seated figure with a log — L. N. Tallur

## Subject & Narrative

A large pale seated figure supports an enormous dark log obliquely across its upper body and head area. A visitor stands at left with a raised hammer-like tool.

## Pose & Gesture

The figure sits with one hand on a knee. The log conceals its face, displacing the usual focal point of a devotional statue.

## Material & Technique

A textured wood-like cylinder contrasts with a pale smooth figure bearing visible seams or cracks.

## Architectural Placement

The work is shown in a white gallery with a small wall text and tool ledge to the right.
