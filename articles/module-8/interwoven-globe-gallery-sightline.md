# Interwoven Globe — gallery sightline

## Physical Arrangement

A large coat-of-arms hanging occupies the left wall beside a doorway. A framed crowned mother-and-child image hangs to the right. Through the central opening, a gold-coloured flowering-vase textile stands nearly on axis in the next room, with a small red garment further right. Low rails hold visitors back from the side works.

## What the Arrangement Communicates

Course reading: The doorway lets viewers compare textile ornament with heraldic and devotional imagery across rooms. The juxtaposition suggests that cloth participates in several systems of identity.

## Objects & Origins
