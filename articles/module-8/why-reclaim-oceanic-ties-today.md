# Why reclaim oceanic ties today?

## Argument

Course reading: The concluding slide asks what connectedness teaches us and places three earlier examples side by side. The visual recap invites a historical answer built from specific forms of exchange rather than a claim of pure, isolated traditions.

## Evidence & Artworks

Course reading: The Vijaya Vitthala warrior column, Ascetic Riding a Nag and Portrait of Père Tanguy recur from Modules 1, 3 and 7. They involve different media, periods and ways of recognizing cultural contact.

## Course Throughline

Course reading: Oceanic ties illuminate trade, craft agency, political identities and artistic recombination. These dimensions remain separately arguable and need not all apply equally to every object.

## Slide Context

### Module 8, slide 20

**Heading context:** Why Reclaiming the Ocean and Oceanic Ties is Important Today?

Course reading: The Vijaya Vitthala warrior, Ascetic Riding a Nag and Père Tanguy form the final recap. Stone carving, a marbled painting and a European portrait surrounded by Japanese prints supply three different histories of connection. Reclaiming oceanic ties means attending to those mechanisms of encounter and agency rather than treating regions as visually isolated.
