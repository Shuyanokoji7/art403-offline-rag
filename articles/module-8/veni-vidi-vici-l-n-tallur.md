## Physical Arrangement

Two long inward-facing sloped banks of terracotta roofing tiles rise on either side of a narrow central passage. Above them, the building’s exposed trusses converge toward the far wall. Repeated horizontal tile courses amplify the depth of the hall.

## What the Arrangement Communicates

Course reading: Tiles become an environment encountered bodily through a corridor, while the roof above repeats their architectural association. The lecture links Mangalore tiles with Swiss-German Christian connections from 1865 onward.

## Objects & Origins

Course reading: Tallur’s 2012 site-specific installation uses Mangalore terracotta tiles and iron at Aspinwall House, Kochi-Muziris Biennale.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The tile installation’s meaning is exhausted by arranging terracotta attractively in a hall.”

**Why it is wrong:** The two sloped banks create a bodily passage and bring Mangalore tile manufacture, mission-industry history and architectural material into a contemporary encounter.
