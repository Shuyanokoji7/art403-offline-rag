# India’s First Biennale — Fort Kochi poster

## Process

Printed publicity posters documented photographically.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The placement on ordinary walls in Fort Kochi makes public promotion part of the biennale’s urban presence. Photographer credits are preserved separately from poster authorship.

## Composition & Motifs

A small white typographic poster is pasted alone onto a weathered wall beneath corrugated roofing. The prominent date reads 12/12/12.

## Slide Context

### Module 8, slide 16

**Heading context:** Kochi Muziris Biennale and Reclaiming Global Ties

Course reading: The Fort Kochi opening poster and two It’s my Biennale portrait designs put an international exhibition into ordinary local streets. Repetition and the first-person slogan connect public ownership with place. The following installations show how the event’s regional identity is developed through particular works and historical connections.
