# Elephant by the shore — framed historical image

## Process

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: The slide’s heading connects the image to Elephanta, excavation and colonial legacy, without specifying this image’s catalogue record.

## Composition & Motifs

A large elephant stands near water, with palm trees and tiny people surrounding it. Two figures sit in the foreground. A pale mount and frame separate it from the contemporary installation view.
