# Eros — Sleeping Cupid, Caravaggio

## Composition

A pale sleeping child lies diagonally across a dark field, with bent limbs and a dark wing rising behind the torso. Strong illumination isolates the body.

## Perspective Convention

Foreshortening gives the sleeping body volume within a compressed dark space.

## Palette & Pigment

Warm flesh highlights emerge from brown-black shadow. The small installation photograph limits pigment and brushwork analysis.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

A curved bow-like object lies beside the reclining figure. Fine attributes are partly obscured by distance and frame.
