# Vallarpadam container terminal — lecture photograph

## Studio vs Documentary Context

Course reading: The lecture uses the terminal photograph as a comparison for The Ship of Tarshish and names Vallarpadam Container Terminal.

## Subject & Evidence

A large modern container ship faces the viewer in water, with stacked coloured containers, dockside cranes and port infrastructure behind.

## Slide Context

### Module 8, slide 17

**Heading context:** Ship of Tarshish, Biblical Ties and Trade

Course reading: Raghavan’s container-ship work connects the Biblical Tarshish reference with contemporary shipping under Biblical Ties and Trade. It follows the street campaign and precedes Tallur’s tile installation. The sequence makes historical narrative, current commerce and local material history different ways of reclaiming global ties.
