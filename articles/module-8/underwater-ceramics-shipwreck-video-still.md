# Underwater ceramics — shipwreck video still

## Studio vs Documentary Context

An underwater video still following the Belitung slide.

## Subject & Evidence

Several large round and oval ceramic vessels lie tipped or partly buried on the seabed. Encrusted surfaces and green-blue water reduce detail.

## Slide Context

### Module 8, slide 11

**Heading context:** Understanding interconnections, mass production and social life of objects through maritime history (continued from slide 9)

Course reading: Belitung bowls arranged as waves around a dhow are compared with underwater documentation and Kerkar’s encrusted cross. The museum uses repeated cargo to give production and shipping a spatial form; the underwater image records another stage of the objects’ lives. The cross introduces the ocean’s material participation in contemporary making.
