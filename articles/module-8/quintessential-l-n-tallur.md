# Quintessential — L. N. Tallur

## Physical Arrangement

A partly cut-away elephant-shaped wooden form occupies the dark room, surrounded by many detached rectangular and curved blocks on the floor. A large four-part video display hangs behind and above. Two pale busts on plinths flank the rear. A small framed image on the left wall echoes the separately enlarged historical picture.

## What the Arrangement Communicates

Course reading: The juxtaposition of intact image, dismantled body, museum busts and surveillance-like video stages a tension between preserving, observing and fragmenting an object. This is an inference from the arrangement, connected by the heading to Elephanta and colonial legacy.

## Slide Context

### Module 8, slide 19

**Heading context:** Archaeological Excavation in Elephanta and Colonial Legacy Reimagined

Course reading: The framed elephant image is placed in relation to Quintessential’s screen, sculptural objects and fragmented forms. Under the Elephanta and colonial-legacy heading, archival representation and contemporary installation rework historical authority. Precise spatial relationships supply the curatorial argument, not the mere fact that several objects share a room.
