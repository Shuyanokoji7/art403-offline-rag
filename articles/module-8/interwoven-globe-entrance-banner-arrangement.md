# Interwoven Globe — entrance banner arrangement

## Physical Arrangement

A wide title strip crosses a grid of different textile patterns. Below it, garment mannequins and a small information panel sit against deep red sections. The display is recessed within an architectural frame.

## What the Arrangement Communicates

Course reading: The juxtaposed patterns and garments introduce variety within a shared worldwide textile-trade story. This is a reading of the visible grouping.

## Objects & Origins

Course reading: The banner names the worldwide textile trade, 1500–1800. Individual cloth and garment labels are too small to identify.
