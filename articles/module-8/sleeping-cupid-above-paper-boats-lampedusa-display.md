# Sleeping Cupid above paper boats — Lampedusa display

## Physical Arrangement

The framed Caravaggio hangs high in a dark blue wall recess under focused light. Numerous small folded paper boats spread irregularly across the floor beneath, separated from visitors by a low rope. A large vertical interval divides painting and boats.

## What the Arrangement Communicates

Course reading: The elevated painting and scattered paper boats connect historical art with a Mediterranean setting. The museum’s trust-and-dialogue title gives this vertical juxtaposition a contemporary social reading.

## Objects & Origins

Course reading: Caravaggio’s Eros of 1608 is captioned within the Lampedusa 2016 exhibition. The floor objects belong to the installation context.

## Distinctions
