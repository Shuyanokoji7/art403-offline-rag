# Charminar

## Plan & Spatial Organization

Module 3 shows Charminar in the distance through a foreground Char Kaman gateway. The two structures must be distinguished.

## Structural System

Large pointed arches are the main visible spanning vocabulary. The close detail in Module 1 shows bracketed projections and nested arch mouldings.

## Elevation & Massing

Tall minarets rise beyond the foreground gateway. The close-up reveals stacked ornamented façade bands.

## Ornament Vocabulary

Round floral medallions, pendant bud-like forms, repeated brackets and dense stucco borders articulate the façade.

## Syncretism

Course reading: The explicit comparison is with Lotus Mahal’s stucco, demonstrating a shared regional ornamental discussion.
