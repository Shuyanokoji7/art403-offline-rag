# Krishna Temple — sculpted hall at Hampi

## Structural System

Stone posts support horizontal beams and projecting brackets: a visibly trabeated hall.

## Elevation & Massing

Repeated columns and a low horizontal eave create a receding sequence of bays.

## Ornament Vocabulary

Rearing composite animals project from the columns. Elephants flank the foreground steps. Smaller figural reliefs occupy the column shafts.

## Materials & Construction Technique

Carved stone members combine structural support with relief and nearly freestanding animal bodies.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The Krishna Temple hall carries its roof on a sequence of true arches.”

**Why it is wrong:** The elephant sculptures beside the steps and the composite creatures on the pillars are different elements; the hall is not supported by true arches.
