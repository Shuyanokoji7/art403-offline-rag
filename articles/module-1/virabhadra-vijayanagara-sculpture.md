# Virabhadra — Vijayanagara sculpture

## Subject & Narrative

Course reading: The caption identifies Virabhadra, a martial divine figure.

## Iconographic Attributes

Tall cylindrical crown, large earrings, layered jewellery and an upward-pointing sword are visible.

## Pose & Gesture

An upright frontal stance contrasts with Badami’s extended diagonal leg. The arms and implements are contained inside a tall ornamental arch.

## Material & Technique

A carved stone slab with openings around parts of the body and upper arms, shown on a museum support.

## Slide Context

### Module 1, slide 8

**Heading context:** How do we understand/compare sculptural styles?

Course reading: Badami’s expansive Vamana figure is paired with the upright, framed Virabhadra sculpture to compare sculptural styles. Pose, bodily containment and surrounding figures supply the comparison; the deities remain different. The preceding Krishna Temple hall and following royal platform place this stylistic exercise within Hampi’s wider architectural enquiry.
