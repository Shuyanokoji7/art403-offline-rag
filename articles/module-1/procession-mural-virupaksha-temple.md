# Procession mural — Virupaksha Temple

## Composition

Horizontal narrative bands are separated by dark floral borders. Chariot-like forms and clustered attendants move laterally through the middle band. Larger figures fill the lower band.

## Perspective Convention

Figures and vehicles stack in shallow registers. There is no consistent single vanishing point.

## Palette & Pigment

A worn red, cream and dark palette remains visible.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

Large wheels and canopied vehicles appear in the middle register. A conspicuous frontal face occupies the far lower right.
