# Krishnadevaraya — Tirumala bronze

## Subject & Narrative

Course reading: The caption names the centre figure within the royal group at Tirumala.

The three figures face forward with hands joined at the chest.

## Pose & Gesture

Course reading: Joined palms make devotion publicly visible.

All stand on individual circular bases. The central ruler is taller and wears a high head covering.

## Material & Technique

Course reading: The caption identifies bronze.

Reflective surfaces, jewellery and garment bands are visible.

## Architectural Placement

Installed royal group within Tirumala Temple, not portraits painted on a manuscript page.

## Slide Context

### Module 1, slide 20

**Heading context:** Figure of the King

Course reading: The three Tirumala bronzes of Krishnadevaraya and his queens are grouped with the Lepakshi mural of kabai and kullayi dress. Courtly clothing and devotional posture connect royal identity to sacred representation across bronze and paint. The preceding procession and following temple carvings locate the ruler within a broader visual programme.
