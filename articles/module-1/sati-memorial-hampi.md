# Sati memorial — Hampi

## Subject & Narrative

Two standing figures occupy one principal field beneath a rounded upper edge. The figure at right raises an arm.

## Pose & Gesture

The left figure holds hands together at the chest. The right figure’s raised hand creates the main asymmetry.

## Material & Technique

Shallow carving on a stone slab. Details are weathered.

## Architectural Placement

Shown as a detached memorial on a labelled museum plinth.
