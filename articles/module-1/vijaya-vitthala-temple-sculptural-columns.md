# Vijaya Vitthala Temple — sculptural columns

## Iconographic Attributes

A rearing animal with curved trunk, mane and large round patterned haunch projects at right. Smaller human reliefs occupy adjacent shafts.

## Pose & Gesture

Raised forelegs and curved bodies animate an otherwise vertical system of columns and horizontal beams.

## Material & Technique

The animals are carved in high relief against structural stone supports.

## Architectural Placement

Composite carved pillars frame a view toward the stone chariot in the courtyard.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The composite animals on the Vijaya Vitthala columns are decorative pictures unrelated to the supports.”

**Why it is wrong:** The animal forms project from the carved columns within a repeated structural bay system; ornament and support are encountered together.
