# Vamana Avatara of Vishnu — Badami relief

## Subject & Narrative

Course reading: The lecture names Vamana.

The image emphasizes a multi-armed figure with one leg extended high across the upper field, the expanded cosmic-stride form rather than an isolated small dwarf.

## Iconographic Attributes

Course reading: Tall headdress and multiple arms mark divinity.

## Pose & Gesture

A strong diagonal lifted leg and radiating arms surround the central upright body. Subsidiary figures occupy the lower right.

## Architectural Placement

Cut into a cave wall beside a carved column, unlike the separate slab shown at right.

## Slide Context

### Module 1, slide 8

**Heading context:** How do we understand/compare sculptural styles?

Course reading: Badami’s expansive Vamana figure is paired with the upright, framed Virabhadra sculpture to compare sculptural styles. Pose, bodily containment and surrounding figures supply the comparison; the deities remain different. The preceding Krishna Temple hall and following royal platform place this stylistic exercise within Hampi’s wider architectural enquiry.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The Badami Vamana is a small upright dwarf contained within a narrow frame.”

**Why it is wrong:** Vamana’s narrative and the larger cosmic-stride manifestation should not be reduced to the claim that every image of Vamana is a small standing figure.
