# Stepped tank or Kalyani — Hampi

## Building Type & Function

Course reading: A stepped water tank adjacent in the lecture’s discussion to the royal platform and Rama Temple.

## Plan & Spatial Organization

A square basin descends through repeated zigzag flights of steps on its sides to a smaller square of water.

## Structural System

Stepped retaining masonry, not a dome or columned hall.

## Materials & Construction Technique

Regular stone blocks create an unusually crisp geometric descent.

## Slide Context

### Module 1, slide 12

**Heading context:** Proximity to the stepped tank and Rama Temple (early Vijayanagara structure)

Course reading: The stepped tank is paired with the Rama Temple relief of festival and warfare. The juxtaposition asks how water architecture, movement and ritual imagery might relate to Narasimha and Vasantotsava. This is the lecture’s posed connection, while the preceding royal platform supplies the political and ceremonial setting.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The stepped tank’s proximity to the Rama Temple establishes that Vasantotsava was its sole ritual use.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: Proximity to the Rama Temple does not by itself prove a particular ritual use of the tank; the Vasantotsava connection remains interrogative.
