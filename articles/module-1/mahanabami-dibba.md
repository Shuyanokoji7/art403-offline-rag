# Mahanabami Dibba

## Building Type & Function

Course reading: A royal ceremonial platform, connected in the lecture to the Mahanabami festival.

## Plan & Spatial Organization

Three diminishing tiers form a broad raised platform. Slide 10 explicitly locates stairs on east, west and south. The map on 9 locates it in the Royal Centre.

## Structural System

Massive stepped masonry platform. Neither a post-and-lintel hall nor an arch-and-dome span is the relevant system for the surviving base.

## Ornament Vocabulary

Horizontal registers contain elephants, horses, people, warriors and other animals.

Course reading: Slide 10 singles out Narasimha and Chenchu Lakshmi as religious exceptions to the largely non-divine imagery.

## Materials & Construction Technique

Course reading: Slide 9 identifies granite.

Slide 15 distinguishes an early granite phase and later green chlorite facing.

## Syncretism

Course reading: Foreign-looking costume and traded animals belong to the interregional imagery argument.

Course reading: The royal platform brings ceremony and military display together.

Course reading: Horse handlers and interregional figures connect royal spectacle to trade.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The Mahanabami Dibba’s imagery is exclusively secular, with no religious dimension.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: The royal ceremonial and martial imagery includes Narasimha and Chenchu Lakshmi. An answer excluding the religious/cosmological dimension leaves out named figures in the programme.
