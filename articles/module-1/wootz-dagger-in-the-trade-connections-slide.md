# Wootz dagger in the trade-connections slide

## Technique

Course reading: The slide identifies wootz steel.

Long channels and reflected highlights are visible.

## Form & Function

A broad triangular blade tapers to a point. Two long parallel side bars and cross grips give it a push-dagger form.

## Decorative Vocabulary

A relatively undecorated dark grip contrasts with the bright blade.
