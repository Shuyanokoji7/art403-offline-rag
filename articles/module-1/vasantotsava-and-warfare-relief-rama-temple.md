# Vasantotsava and warfare relief — Rama Temple

## Subject & Narrative

Course reading: Captioned as Vasantotsava and warfare.

Horizontal bands combine processional animals, standing participants, active confrontations and carried or wheeled structures.

## Pose & Gesture

Bent knees, raised arms and opposing figures give the lower registers a more animated rhythm than the repeated animals above.

## Material & Technique

Low stone relief arranged across masonry joints.

## Architectural Placement

Exterior wall friezes at the Rama Temple, not a detached museum panel.

## Slide Context

### Module 1, slide 12

**Heading context:** Proximity to the stepped tank and Rama Temple (early Vijayanagara structure)

Course reading: The stepped tank is paired with the Rama Temple relief of festival and warfare. The juxtaposition asks how water architecture, movement and ritual imagery might relate to Narasimha and Vasantotsava. This is the lecture’s posed connection, while the preceding royal platform supplies the political and ceremonial setting.
