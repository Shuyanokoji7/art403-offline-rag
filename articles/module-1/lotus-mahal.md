# Lotus Mahal

## Building Type & Function

Course reading: Palatial pavilion in Hampi’s royal quarter.

## Plan & Spatial Organization

The diagram shows a central grid with projections on four sides. The section distinguishes two storeys and a higher central roof.

## Structural System

Repeated cusped arches sit beneath horizontal eaves and stepped roof masses.

Course reading: The lecture compares this arcuate vocabulary with temple and palatial tower forms.

## Elevation & Massing

A dominant pyramidal central tower is surrounded by lower stepped towers. Deep overhanging eaves separate the levels.

## Ornament Vocabulary

Slide 24 reveals layered arch mouldings, lace-like scrollwork and a large mask-like face directly above the central cusp.

## Syncretism

Course reading: The lecture explicitly joins Hindu temple/palatial pyramidal forms with Islamic arch and stucco vocabularies in one structure.

## Slide Context

**Heading context:** Amalgamation of Hindu Temple/Palatial and Islamic Architecture in Lotus Mahal

Course reading: The Lotus Mahal combines arches, posts, projecting eaves and tiered roof forms under an explicit heading about temple, palatial and Islamic architecture.
