# Hero stone — Hampi

## Subject & Narrative

Three registers separate an active lower scene, a middle group and an upper devotional-looking scene.

## Pose & Gesture

The bottom figures bend and extend limbs dynamically, unlike the frontal standing pair on the sati memorial.

## Material & Technique

Weathered shallow stone relief.

## Architectural Placement

A detached memorial slab shown on a museum support and repeated in the final recap.

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: The lecture compares a separate memorial stone with platform carving. Shared registers and heroic action connect the two objects without relocating the memorial onto the platform.
