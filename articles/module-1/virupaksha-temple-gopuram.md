# Virupaksha Temple gopuram

## Building Type & Function

Course reading: A monumental temple gateway, rather than the main sanctuary tower.

## Structural System

The courtyard’s visible column-and-beam galleries are trabeated.

## Elevation & Massing

Successively narrower storeys rise above a broad base. Dense miniature architectural units and figures cover the pale upper structure.

## Ornament Vocabulary

Tiered aedicule-like forms and figural ornament articulate the tower.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The Virupaksha gopuram is the superstructure directly over the temple’s main deity.”

**Why it is wrong:** Its role is a monumental entrance tower. The paired mural connects processional imagery to a temple setting, not to a claim that every tower marks a sanctuary.
