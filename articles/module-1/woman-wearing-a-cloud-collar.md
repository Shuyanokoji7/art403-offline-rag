# Woman wearing a cloud collar

## Composition

An isolated upper-body figure is placed against a pale ground, with a faint circular line around the head.

## Palette & Pigment

Strong red and yellow garments contrast with a pale blue-white lobed collar.

## Figural Style

Jewelled headdress, dark hair and a broad scalloped shoulder collar supply the comparative costume evidence.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

The cloud-like lobes encircle the neck and shoulders. A large sign appears on the central red garment.
