# Vijaya Vitthala Temple — sculptural friezes

## Subject & Narrative

The lower photograph shows elephants alternating with attendants in a long low band, with additional animals and miniature shrines above.

## Pose & Gesture

Repeated profiles and walking legs make lateral movement legible.

## Material & Technique

Stone carving varies between shallow ornamental strips and more projecting elephants.

## Architectural Placement

Friezes wrap the base and stepped projection of the temple architecture.

## Slide Context

### Module 1, slide 21

**Heading context:** Interregional imagery in other structures of Hampi

Course reading: Vijaya Vitthala friezes, animal-bearing columns and three warrior panels extend the enquiry into interregional imagery beyond the royal platform. Compare clothing, weapons, pose and composite animals within their architectural positions. Differences among the three warriors matter: they are separate carved figures, not duplicate views of one person.
