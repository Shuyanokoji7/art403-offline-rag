# Vijaya Vitthala warrior — centre relief

## Iconographic Attributes

The rider reclines against the rearing animal’s side, with a long weapon angled toward its head. The creature’s curling trunk and open mouth are particularly clear.

## Material & Technique

Carved stone, with deeply modelled animal bodies and smaller human figures.

## Architectural Placement

A figure integrated into a composite animal pillar in the Mahamandapa, Vijaya Vitthala Temple.

## Costume & Ethnic Markers

Course reading: The collective caption supplies Portuguese, Arab (?) and Indigenous warrior as the three identities in the comparison. The question mark belongs to the lecture’s Arab identification.
