# Golconda Coverlet — central pavilion

## Technique

Course reading: The caption identifies the coverlet as kalamkari.

## Field & Border Organization

A central multi-tiered pavilion divides a roughly square field. A seated figure is beneath its canopy. Standing attendants and birds occupy the sides and upper corners. Several narrow patterned borders frame the field.

## Dyes & Materials

Red, green, dark outlines and a pale ground organize the design.

## Function

Course reading: Captioned coverlet. The architecture belongs to the depicted scene rather than the textile’s physical support.

## Circulation

Course reading: The identical pavilion, seated pair, surrounding figures and borders establish the cross-module image match. Module 2 provides National Museum accession 48.7-103.

## Motifs & Locations

Module 2 slide 14 enlarges the lower-left standing figures and a spiky fruit beside them.

Course reading: Its heading identifies pineapple.

The full view also contains birds above the roof and large flowering plants beside attendants.
