# Later green-chlorite friezes — Mahanabami Dibba

## Subject & Narrative

Elephants occupy the lowest visible figured band, with animals and groups of people above.

## Material & Technique

Course reading: Captioned as later green chlorite.

Deeper modelling, finer scrolling borders and more projecting mouldings are visible than in the paired granite detail.

## Architectural Placement

Facing courses on the ceremonial platform, separated by ornamental mouldings.

## Slide Context

### Module 1, slide 15

**Heading context:** Phases of Development of the Platform

Course reading: The early granite carving and later green-chlorite facing are placed together as phases of the Mahanabami Dibba.
