# Paigah Tombs

## Building Type & Function

Course reading: Funerary complex identified by the lecture caption in Hyderabad.

## Structural System

A broad cusped arch frames the view. Its lobed silhouette is an arcuate vocabulary.

## Elevation & Massing

A horizontal parapet is punctuated by bulbous, clustered finials and slender vertical spikes.

## Ornament Vocabulary

Pierced scrolling bands and tightly repeated floral details contrast with the large unornamented arch opening.

## Slide Context

### Module 1, slide 2

**Heading context:** Ebb and Flow: Art and Globalism of Oceanic Networks (continued from slide 1)

Course reading: The cover pairs a floral ceramic dish and floral fabric with a stone trader-and-horses relief under Ebb and Flow: Art and Globalism of Oceanic Networks. Repeated flowers connect ceramic and textile; the relief contributes the people and traded animals. This cross-media grouping argues for the circulation of goods, designs and artisanal knowledge through contact. The Paigah Tombs and Coromandel kalamkari hanging on slides 2–3 extend that argument into architectural ornament, textile making and human interaction.
