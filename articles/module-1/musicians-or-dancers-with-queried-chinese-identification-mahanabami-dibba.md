# Musicians or dancers with queried Chinese identification — Mahanabami Dibba

## Subject & Narrative

Slide 17 isolates two performers. Slide 18 restores them to a multi-register group including animals and additional performers.

## Pose & Gesture

The left figure bends one arm toward the head. The right figure crosses bent arms across the chest. Flexed knees and tilted heads create dancing motion.

## Architectural Placement

Low relief in the platform’s outer registers.

## Costume & Ethnic Markers

Flared garments, head coverings and broad shoulder contours motivate the comparison.

Course reading: Chinese musicians (?) remains the lecture’s tentative label.
