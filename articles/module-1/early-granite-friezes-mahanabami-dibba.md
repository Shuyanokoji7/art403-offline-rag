# Early granite friezes — Mahanabami Dibba

## Subject & Narrative

Elephants with lowered trunks occupy a prominent middle band. Horses and handlers run below. Smaller riders and animals appear in the upper bands.

## Pose & Gesture

Profile animals walk laterally while small human figures animate the intervals.

## Material & Technique

Course reading: The lecture identifies the early carvings as granite.

Broad bodies, shallow outlines and visible block boundaries distinguish them from the finer later facing.

## Architectural Placement

Horizontal outer-face registers on the platform.
