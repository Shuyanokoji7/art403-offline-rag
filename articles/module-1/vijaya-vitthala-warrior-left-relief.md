# Vijaya Vitthala warrior — left relief

## Iconographic Attributes

The small rider wears a brimmed or rounded cap and fitted upper garment. A long blade rises beside the head. The larger animal has a patterned haunch and curled mane.

## Material & Technique

Carved stone, with deeply modelled animal bodies and smaller human figures.

## Architectural Placement

A figure integrated into a composite animal pillar in the Mahamandapa, Vijaya Vitthala Temple.

## Costume & Ethnic Markers

Course reading: The collective caption supplies Portuguese, Arab (?) and Indigenous warrior as the three identities in the comparison. The question mark belongs to the lecture’s Arab identification.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The three Vijaya Vitthala warrior panels are repeated views of one carved figure.”

**Why it is wrong:** The three panels contain different warrior figures, costumes and accompanying forms. Their juxtaposition is a comparison within a shared temple context.
