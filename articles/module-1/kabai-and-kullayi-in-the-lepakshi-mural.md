# Kabai and kullayi in the Lepakshi mural

## Composition

A row of standing male figures occupies a shallow orange field beneath hanging ornament. A patterned lower band closes the scene.

## Perspective Convention

Figures overlap slightly but share a shallow band rather than a deep architectural space.

## Line & Hand

Dark outlines articulate profiles and garment edges. The photograph is too worn to assign separate hands.

## Figural Style

Long pale robes, tall pointed hats, patterned lower garments and joined hands dominate the group. The slide names the robe and cap kabai and kullayi.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The Lepakshi kabai and kullayi prove that the wearers converted to Islam.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: The robe and cap participate in an adopted courtly dress vocabulary. A clothing form connects political presentation and cultural exchange; it does not establish conversion.
