# Court dress or dodot — TAPI 00.187

## Technique

Course reading: The embedded label explicitly gives cotton, block-printing, painted mordant and resist-dyeing.

These are complementary stages, not mutually exclusive alternatives.

## Field & Border Organization

A large oval patchwork-like field combines small triangular and square patterns within a floral ground.

Course reading: The label describes a printed and dyed cotton design.

## Function

Course reading: The label identifies an elite Javanese hip-wrapper. Its additional discussion of ceremonial uses concerns dodots as a garment category.

## Circulation

Course reading: Made on the Coromandel Coast, traded to Indonesia and used in Java. Making place and use place differ.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The dodot was manufactured in Java because Javanese court elites wore it.”

**Why it is wrong:** A textile used in Java is not necessarily manufactured there.
