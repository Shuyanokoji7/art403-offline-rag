# Dyed and printed Coromandel hanging — ROM 2000.93.1

## Technique

Course reading: Captioned dyed and printed cotton.

The slide asks how many colours are visible, without giving a definitive number of blocks.

## Field & Border Organization

A large pointed pendant-like form hangs inside a cusped arch-shaped field. Narrow side panels repeat elongated ornaments. Multiple floral borders enclose the rectangle.

## Dyes & Materials

Blue in the upper corners, red motifs, pale ground and dark outlines are visible. Shades and ageing prevent a reliable technical colour count.

## Function

Course reading: A hanging with an architectural niche format.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The number of colours visible in the hanging equals the number of printing blocks used.”

**Why it is wrong:** Visible colours are the result of the making sequence. Overlap, reserves and dye stages prevent a one-colour-to-one-block inference from the finished hanging.
