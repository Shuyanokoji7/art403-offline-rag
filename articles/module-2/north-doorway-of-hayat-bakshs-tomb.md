# North doorway of Hayat Baksh’s tomb

## Building Type & Function

Course reading: Tomb doorway in the Qutb Shahi complex, Hyderabad.

## Plan & Spatial Organization

A rectangular door is set within a decorated surround beneath a much larger pointed arch. A small upper recess aligns with the door centre.

## Structural System

The image juxtaposes a rectangular lintelled doorway and an enclosing pointed arch. These are different scales of framing.

## Ornament Vocabulary

A repeating row of crowned oval fruit-like forms runs across the lintel zone.

Course reading: The slide heading identifies pineapple.

Floral and geometric bands surround the door.
