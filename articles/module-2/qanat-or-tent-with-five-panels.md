# Qanat or tent with five panels

## Technique

Course reading: Dyed and painted cotton, as captioned.

The full textile is held horizontally for photography.

## Field & Border Organization

Five pointed-arch panels form a continuous wall-like sequence beneath a broad horizontal border. The central panel contains a two-headed bird with a large upward-spreading tail or plumage. Vegetal forms fill adjacent panels.

## Function

Course reading: Qanat or tent panel: portable fabric architecture rather than a stone façade.

## Circulation

Course reading: Deccan production. National Museum, New Delhi, accession 48.7/29. The date comes from the matched detail on slide 7.

## Motifs & Locations

The bird’s two necks curve outward below its central body. Small animals and floral scrolls occupy the surrounding field. Slide 7 isolates this central panel.
