# Mihrab at the Jama Masjid — Bijapur

## Building Type & Function

Course reading: A mosque prayer niche, captioned Mehrab.

## Structural System

Nested pointed arches articulate the recess.

## Ornament Vocabulary

Gold-coloured decoration on a dark ground includes calligraphic bands, miniature architectural forms and hanging lamp-like motifs. White surrounding vaulting frames the darker niche.

## Syncretism

Course reading: The lecture’s comparison is with patterned textiles and Deccani architecture, not a claim that every motif belongs exclusively to one religion.
