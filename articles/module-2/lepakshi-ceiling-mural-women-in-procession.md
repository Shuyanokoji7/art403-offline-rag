# Lepakshi ceiling mural — women in procession

## Composition

A row of women advances across a warm ochre field, framed above and below by patterned bands. Overlapping bodies remain shallow in depth.

## Line & Hand

Fine contours delineate faces, ornaments and textiles despite areas of wear.

## Figural Style

Profile faces, large eyes, dark hair bunches, layered jewellery and differently patterned cloth distinguish the figures.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

Repeated pale hanging shapes with dark dotted patterns appear above the women. Floral roundels occupy the borders.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The Lepakshi procession and Golconda hanging are painted on the same kind of support.”

**Why it is wrong:** Lepakshi is a wall/ceiling painting and the Golconda hanging is cloth. Processional figures and costume connect their imagery across those different supports.
