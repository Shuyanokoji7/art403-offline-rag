# Artisan drawing kalamkari at Srikalahasti

## Studio vs Documentary Context

A process photograph. A large peacock-like bird with a fanned tail fills the cloth. Outlined areas and a dark filled ground coexist.

## Subject & Evidence

An overhead view shows an artisan seated on cloth, working near its lower edge. A dark-liquid container is below. A weight holds the upper edge. Handwork, control of outlines and filling, and the textile’s scale relative to the body are directly visible. The dye’s chemical identity is not.
