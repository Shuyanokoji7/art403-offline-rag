# Ramayana textile — Panchakalla Peda Subbarayudu

## Technique

Course reading: A painted narrative textile as identified in the lecture’s kalamkari sequence.

## Field & Border Organization

Concentric rectangular narrative bands surround a central enthroned group. Figures on the upper and side bands turn with the route around the centre, requiring rotational reading rather than one fixed upright view.

## Function

Course reading: Religious epic narration on cloth. The caption names the Ramayana and the maker.

## Circulation

Course reading: Chirala production and V&A accession IS2103-1883 identify making and current collection separately.

## Motifs & Locations

Course reading: Battle groups, chariots, processions, trees and divine figures populate successive compartments.

The centre gathers a larger seated group beneath a canopy.
