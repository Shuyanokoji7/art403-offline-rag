# Two-headed bird on a Dutch-cemetery gravestone

## Subject & Narrative

A carved heraldic arrangement includes a divided shield, scrolling surrounds and an upper two-headed bird-like crest.

## Iconographic Attributes

One half of the shield contains a bird form. The other contains lozenge-shaped elements. A floral oval border encloses the composition.

## Material & Technique

Low stone relief with much finer scroll carving than a simple incised outline.

## Architectural Placement

Course reading: Funerary stone at the Dutch cemetery, Walandapalem, Machilipatnam, according to the caption.
