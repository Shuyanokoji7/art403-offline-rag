# Golconda Hanging — Calico Museum 647

## Technique

Course reading: Dyed and painted cotton, captioned possibly Pulicut.

The question mark on place of production is retained.

## Field & Border Organization

The visible portion contrasts two oversized standing men at left with smaller courtly pairs in vertically stacked arched niches at right. Red grounds and dense floral borders bind the compartments.

## Circulation

Course reading: Calico Museum, Ahmedabad, accession 647. It is distinct from the National Museum coverlet 48.7-103.

## Motifs & Locations

Patterned turbans and robes dominate the large figures. Hanging lamp-like forms appear above. Flowering branches divide the smaller scenes.
