# Manikkavachar and Meenakshi temple hanging

## Field & Border Organization

Long horizontal narrative registers cross a dark red ground. Thin patterned separators and a border of animals enclose the scenes.

## Function

Course reading: The caption identifies the life of Manikkavachar and the wedding of Meenakshi with Sundereswar: a temple narrative programme.

## Circulation

Course reading: Madurai. Royal Ontario Museum accession 972.117.7.

## Motifs & Locations

Processions and vehicles appear in the upper bands. Groups of gesturing figures fill lower bands. Large animal forms line the lower border.

## Slide Context

### Module 2, slide 13

**Heading context:** How do we differentiate these kalamkari works?

Course reading: The Golconda coverlet is paired with the hanging of Manikkavachar and Meenakshi’s wedding. Courtly imagery and religious narrative distinguish the two textiles even where borders and repeated figures invite formal comparison. The heading asks how to differentiate kalamkari works, not how to treat them as one subject.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The Manikkavachar–Meenakshi hanging and Golconda coverlet have the same narrative subject.”

**Why it is wrong:** Shared kalamkari practice does not imply identical themes, dates or viewing formats.
