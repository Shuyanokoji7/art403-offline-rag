# Coromandel textile networks and changing uses

## Argument

Course reading: Slides 2, 10, 15 and 16 connect centres of production, maritime routes, warehouses and different overseas uses. The same technical capacity can serve a Javanese court and European fashion without identical meanings.

## Evidence & Artworks

Course reading: The maps, Dutch warehouse, dodot and chintz garments supply complementary geographical and material evidence.

## Course Throughline

Course reading: Circulation includes artisan skill, commodity exchange, local reuse and changes of social value.

### Module 2, slide 16

**Heading context:** Utilisation of Kalamkari in Europe

Course reading: The chintz jacket and petticoat form a coordinated European dress ensemble. Following the Javanese dodot, they show Indian cotton entering distinct overseas clothing practices. Shared textile circulation therefore produces different social uses and silhouettes, rather than one uniform export style.
