# Manga malai necklace with Gandaberunda

## Technique

Gold-coloured setting with closely spaced red, green and pale insets and small pendant beads.

## Form & Function

A broad pendant suspended from two cords. A pair of outward-facing bird heads crowns the upper edge.

## Decorative Vocabulary

Course reading: The lecture names Gandaberunda.

Mirrored heads, curved necks and repeated feather-like rows make bilateral symmetry explicit.

## Slide Context

### Module 2, slide 7

**Heading context:** Relationship between kalamkari, architecture and Deccani craft

Course reading: The five-panel tent and enlarged two-headed bird are compared with a Manga malai necklace and a Dutch-cemetery gravestone. The Gandaberunda form connects textile, jewellery and carved stone across different uses. The grouping demonstrates motif mobility; the ritual, military and commemorative functions must still be distinguished.
