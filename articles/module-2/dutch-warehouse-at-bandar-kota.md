# Dutch warehouse at Bandar Kota

## Building Type & Function

Course reading: Warehouse beside the Dutch fort at Machilipatnam, captioned seventeenth century.

## Plan & Spatial Organization

An elongated low building is seen obliquely.

## Elevation & Massing

A steep pitched roof meets a plain gabled end with a small upper opening. Side walls are divided by projecting supports.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The Dutch warehouse identifies Dutch workers as the makers of all textiles traded through it.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: The warehouse represents commercial infrastructure. Trading, collecting and physically making a textile are distinct roles; the lecture’s production demonstrations identify craft activity separately.
