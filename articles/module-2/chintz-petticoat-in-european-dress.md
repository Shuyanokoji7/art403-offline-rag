# Chintz petticoat in European dress

## Technique

Course reading: Captioned chintz in a discussion of Indian water-based dyeing.

## Field & Border Organization

Large red and blue flowers and broad winding stems spread across a full gathered skirt. A narrower floral border finishes the hem.

## Function

Course reading: European fashionable dress shown on a mannequin. Jacket and petticoat are separately indexed components.

## Circulation

Course reading: The lecture links Indian cottons to European fashion, botanical interests and the ban on calico.

## Slide Context

### Module 2, slide 16

**Heading context:** Utilisation of Kalamkari in Europe

Course reading: The chintz jacket and petticoat form a coordinated European dress ensemble. Following the Javanese dodot, they show Indian cotton entering distinct overseas clothing practices. Shared textile circulation therefore produces different social uses and silhouettes, rather than one uniform export style.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The chintz petticoat was manufactured entirely in Europe because it forms part of European dress.”

**Why it is wrong:** Indian fabric and European garment construction are separate aspects of origin; neither cancels the other.
