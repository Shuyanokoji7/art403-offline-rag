# Palampore hanging — ROM 934.4.3

## Technique

Course reading: The caption identifies dyed and painted cotton.

The whole object and a magnified lower detail are juxtaposed.

## Field & Border Organization

A flowering tree rises from a mound in a large cream field. Its asymmetric branching is enclosed by a wide rectangular floral border with concentrated corner bouquets.

## Dyes & Materials

Red, blue, brown and yellowish areas are visible.

Course reading: The slide identifies the material as cotton.

## Circulation

Course reading: Southern Indian production and present ROM collection are different locations, not interchangeable provenance claims.

## Motifs & Locations

At the bottom, a tiny hunting scene sits among patterned hills. The enlargement reveals two deer-like animals at left, a spotted quadruped near the centre, and a running figure carrying a pole and suspended load at right. Birds perch along the larger tree.
