# Qutb Shahi gateway facing Golconda fort

## Building Type & Function

Course reading: Gateway identified by the caption. Only the upper opening and decoration are shown.

## Structural System

A large pointed arch is framed by several receding mouldings: arcuate form.

## Ornament Vocabulary

Two large round medallions flank the opening. Smaller saddled quadrupeds sit just above the arch slopes. Paired birds and a central pendant-like ornament appear above the apex. Dense scrolling stucco fills the upper band.

## Materials & Construction Technique

The surface relief and pale plaster are visible. The masonry core is not.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The gateway’s architectural ornament establishes that the kalamkari works were made from its building materials.”

**Why it is wrong:** The comparison follows framing, vegetal and animal ornament between architecture and textile. It does not transfer masonry materials into the cloth-making process.
