# Map of Coromandel — d’Anville

## Process

A historical printed map reproduced on the slide.

## Edition & Circulation

Course reading: Captioned J. B. Bourguignon Anville, 1753, from the David Rumsey collection. The world-route diagram beside it is a separate modern explanatory map.

## Composition & Motifs

The coastline runs down a tall narrow sheet. Dense coastal place names and radiating maritime lines occupy the field. Modern red labels single out Machilipatnam, Pulicut and Nagapattanam.
