# Collecting portraits into albums

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: The lecture’s Witsen example shows a portrait framed by European-language annotation and numbering. Mughal album histories provide comparative evidence that assembling and reframing images is an active practice. Original production, later mounting and overseas collecting are distinct historical acts.

## Slide Context

**M3:22**

Course reading: Khawwas-Khan’s portrait is framed by Witsen’s collecting activity and Dutch commercial connections. The painted courtier, album annotation and European collector are distinct agents. This follows the city-scale discussion by tracing how a Deccani political image entered an overseas collection.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “A collector’s name on an album establishes that collector as the painter of every portrait.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: A collector’s name does not identify the painter or prove that the portrait was originally commissioned for that collection.
