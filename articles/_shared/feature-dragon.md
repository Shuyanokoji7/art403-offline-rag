# Dragon motifs across Asian arts

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Museum teaching material documents movement and transformation of dragon and phoenix motifs between Chinese and Iranian contexts. This supplies a comparative history, not a universal interpretation of every curling creature. Identification still depends on the actual head, limbs and body shown.

## Slide Context

**M7:4**

Course reading: Eisen’s courtesan and Van Gogh’s painted version present a direct source-and-adaptation pair. Figure and costume are retained while palette, border imagery and medium change. The next Père Tanguy portrait extends appropriation into collecting and assembling Japanese images within a European painting.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Every serpentine ornament proves a direct import from China.”

**Why it is wrong:** A serpentine outline does not establish Chinese origin, a particular deity or a direct import.
