# Palampore: furnishing textile

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Museum catalogues identify palampores as large furnishing cloths, including bed coverings. A tree or flowering field is commonly framed by borders in the lecture examples. Function and format should be considered alongside technique and the requirements of a particular market.

## Slide Context

**M2:1**

Course reading: The full palampore and its detail introduce Coromandel textiles through overall field organization and close motif comparison. The production map on slide 2 then connects this object-centred reading to named places and routes, while the later demonstrations reveal the makers’ processes.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Palampore and kalamkari are competing names for the same manufacturing technique.”

**Why it is wrong:** Palampore describes a furnishing category; kalamkari describes making practices. One object can be both.
