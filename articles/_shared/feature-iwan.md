# Iwan and courtyard organization

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: An iwan is a vaulted space open on one side, often addressing a courtyard. The madrasa comparisons invite attention to the large framed opening, its depth and the court it serves, rather than simply treating it as a tall doorway.

## Slide Context

**M3:3, M3:4**

Course reading: Mahmud Gawan Madrasa is compared with Ulugh Beg’s madrasa and the Registan under Timurid Central Asian culture in Deccan. Monumental entrances, towers and patterned surfaces supply the architectural comparison. The next Bidar landscape slide broadens the enquiry to water, gardens and the siting of buildings.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “An iwan is merely a flat ornamental doorway with no spatial depth.”

**Why it is wrong:** An iwan is a spatial unit; a portal is an entrance treatment. Their forms can coincide without the terms being synonyms.
