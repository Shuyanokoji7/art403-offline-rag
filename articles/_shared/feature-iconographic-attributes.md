# Attributes, gestures and identification

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: A deity or spiritual figure can be identified through the combination of held objects, pose, adornment and context. Multiple arms are one representational device, not a diagnosis of one particular deity. Exact attribute combinations matter more than the general category of elaborate figure.

## Slide Context

**M1:8**

Course reading: Badami’s expansive Vamana figure is paired with the upright, framed Virabhadra sculpture to compare sculptural styles. Pose, bodily containment and surrounding figures supply the comparison; the deities remain different. The preceding Krishna Temple hall and following royal platform place this stylistic exercise within Hampi’s wider architectural enquiry.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “A crown and multiple arms identify one particular deity without further attributes.”

**Why it is wrong:** A crown, weapon or many arms alone cannot securely distinguish every deity or identify a human sitter.
