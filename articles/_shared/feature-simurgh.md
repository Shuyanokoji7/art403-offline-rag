# Simurgh and narrative identity

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: In the Shahnameh tradition, the Simurgh protects and raises Zal. A named narrative and its participants support that identification. The course’s fabulous birds should therefore be read with captions and context rather than identified only by size or elaborate plumage.

## Slide Context

**M3:18**

Course reading: The zoomorphic dagger hilt is followed by Sultan Ali Adil Shah I under a question about their relationship. A portable court object and a ruler’s portrait link animal ornament, costume and elite identity. The juxtaposition asks how object culture and represented kingship illuminate one another.

**Why it is wrong:** Simurgh, phoenix and Gandaberunda are not interchangeable names for a fantastic bird.
