# Vanitas and reminders of transience

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Vanitas readings attend to reminders of time, mortality and the instability of possessions. A timepiece or other transience motif supplies a more specific basis than luxury alone. The course’s interpretations should identify the actual object supporting the reading.

## Slide Context

**M4:7**

Course reading: The timepiece and armour still lifes group crafted objects with carefully rendered surfaces. Under the time devoted to making objects discussion, they connect labour and visual attention to possession, transience or status. Different selected objects support different meanings within the still-life format.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Luxury objects alone make every sumptuous still life a vanitas.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: A wealthy display is not automatically a vanitas, and one moral theme does not exhaust its visual or economic significance.
