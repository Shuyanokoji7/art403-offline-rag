# Bidri: contrasting inlay and dark ground

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Bidri combines a darkened metal ground with contrasting inlaid decoration. The technique is associated with Deccani production. Its sharp light-on-dark pattern differs from a surface that is only engraved or painted. Precise alloy proportions require technical documentation.

## Slide Context

**M3:6**

Course reading: The Bidri sailabchi and Iranian ghalamzani vessel compare decorative metalworking across Deccan and Iran. Contrasting inlay on a dark ground and worked surface detail direct attention to different processes. This object-making slide extends the architectural comparison into portable craft.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Bidri decoration is white paint on iron rather than contrasting metal inlay.”

**Why it is wrong:** Bidri inserts contrasting metal into a darkened metal-alloy ground. The bright ornament is inlay; white paint on iron describes a different operation and material combination.
