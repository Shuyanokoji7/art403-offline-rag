# Jina imagery and unresolved print identification

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Museum documentation establishes Jinas as central figures in Jain devotional imagery, with details of dress and attendant figures varying. That comparative knowledge does not settle the identity of the provisionally captioned Pune print in this corpus. Preserve its uncertainty at object level.

## Slide Context

**M6:21**

Course reading: Kali/Tara, the Calcutta Art Studio Buddha and the Pune Mahavir print form a sequence of studio devotional images. Different religious subjects use reproducible colour imagery and commercial publication. The significant distinction is between the regional subject, the technology and the agency of the producing studio.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Any seated meditating figure is securely identifiable as Mahavira.”

**Why it is wrong:** A seated meditating figure is insufficient to distinguish Mahavira from every other Jina or from a Buddhist figure.
