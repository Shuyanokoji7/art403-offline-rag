# Cloud-collar costume and ornamental transfer

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: The cloud collar is a lobed dress form documented in Chinese textiles. Cammann’s study traces a wider ornamental history and changing interpretations. In the Hampi comparison, the collar-like outline is a visual analogy.
