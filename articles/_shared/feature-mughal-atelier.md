# The Mughal atelier and multiple makers

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Akbar’s court supported a workshop combining Persian, Indian and European resources. The course’s paired versions and studies show different forms of adaptation. Attribution to a named painter is distinct from proving that one person completed all drawing, colouring, inscription and mounting.

## Slide Context

**M5:4**

Course reading: Akbar and Adham Khan is used to discuss sartorial choices and a ruler’s legitimacy after the muslin sequence. Clothing operates as a political visual language, not simply surface pattern. ## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “A Mughal atelier attribution means one artist completed drawing, colour, inscription and mounting.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: Attribution to a painter identifies a contribution within a court workshop. Drawing, colouring, inscription and later mounting are distinguishable operations, so one atelier credit does not assign every operation to one person.
