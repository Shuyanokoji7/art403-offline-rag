# Luna: planetary engraving and album reuse

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: The NGV catalogues Beham’s Luna as a 1539 engraving in a planetary series, with a crescent and Cancer sign. The course shows such a European print remounted among Persian drawings. The standalone design’s catalogue is not the provenance of the complete Gulshan composite sheet.

## Slide Context

**M5:18**

Course reading: The Gulshan sheet assembles a Luna engraving with Persian drawings of a seated man, a group of men and a lion. The whole and its components must be read together: collecting, cutting, mounting and framing create a new relationship among previously separate images. One page therefore need not have one maker, date or original medium.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Luna’s date and printmaker apply to every drawing and border on the Gulshan page.”

**Why it is wrong:** The date or authorship of a print fragment does not date or attribute every component of a later album page.
