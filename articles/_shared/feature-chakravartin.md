# Chakravartin: universal sovereignty

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: The wheel-turning universal monarch is a political and religious concept that entered Buddhist imagery. In this corpus it provides a comparative vocabulary for sovereignty, not proof that every ruler represented on a globe bears this exact historical title.

## Slide Context

**M3:20**

Course reading: House of Bijapur brings rulers together in one dynastic composition under Amalgamation of Cultures and Styles. The preceding individual portrait supplies a comparison of scale and political address. A constructed dynastic gathering communicates lineage rather than requiring a single historical sitting.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Every ruler painted on a globe is directly derived from a chakravartin image.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: A conceptual parallel in universal rule does not establish direct derivation of one imperial image from another tradition.
