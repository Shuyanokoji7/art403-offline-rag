# Mihrab and prayer orientation

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: A mihrab marks the prayer direction toward Mecca. Its niche can receive elaborate tilework or inscription. Identifying its directional role is distinct from describing the architectural frame or decorative technique.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “A mihrab is a doorway used to enter the prayer hall.”

**Why it is wrong:** A mihrab marks the prayer direction toward Mecca. Its directional and devotional role distinguishes it from an entrance used to circulate through the building.
