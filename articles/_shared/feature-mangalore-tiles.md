# Mangalore tiles, mission industry and contemporary art

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Tallur’s installation uses roof tiles to connect local material with industrial and colonial histories. The Linden-Museum account links mission tile production to local clay and European technology. The Biennale record documents terracotta tiles and iron in a site-specific installation.

## Slide Context

**M8:18**

Course reading: Tallur’s seated figure with a log is paired with Veni, vidi, vici’s passage between banks of roof tiles. Bodily encounter and material transformation connect the works, while their arrangements remain distinct. The Mangalore tile heading brings mission-industry history into the contemporary installation.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The mission-industry date identifies when Tallur’s tile installation was made.”

**Why it is wrong:** The history of tile manufacture is not the date of Tallur’s installation; a material can carry an earlier history into a contemporary work.
