# Portrait miniatures and close viewing

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Small portable portraits could serve personal and political relationships, including loyalty or affection. Their intimate scale changes how they are held and viewed. A miniature portrait embedded inside a larger painting is also a represented object within that painting’s own argument.

## Slide Context

**M5:23, M5:24**

Course reading: Shah Jahan’s halo, angels and portrait pendant are followed by a comparison with the standing-ruler drawing associated in the lecture with Rembrandt. The sequence tracks courtly self-representation and its reception across media. Maker, represented ruler and later interpreter perform different roles in that connection.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Every small manuscript picture is a European portable portrait miniature.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: Miniature is not a licence to call every small manuscript image a European portrait miniature with the same social function.
