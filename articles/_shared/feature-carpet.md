# Carpets in interiors and trade

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Carpets produced in Islamic-world workshops circulated internationally and could be displayed on tables and walls as well as floors. In European interiors they can indicate valued possessions and pictorial texture. Their exact place of manufacture needs object-specific evidence.

## Slide Context

**M5:2**

Course reading: The Mughal and Iranian animal carpets are placed within the question of continental and maritime trade. Their shared textile format supports comparison of animal imagery and production contexts across regions. The next muslin examples extend the enquiry from patterned furnishing to the material qualities of cloth.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “A carpet painted on a European table must be incorrectly placed floor furnishing.”

**Why it is wrong:** A table carpet is not an incorrectly placed floor covering, and a painted carpet is not a surviving textile specimen.
