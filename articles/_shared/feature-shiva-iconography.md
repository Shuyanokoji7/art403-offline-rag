# Shiva-related imagery in the lecture

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: The lecture captions identify Shiva-related subjects in popular prints and paintings. The Kali/Tara catalogue also identifies Shiva in subordinate positions within that particular diptych. Other linked images must be read through their own captions and attributes rather than borrowing the diptych’s narrative.

## Slide Context

**M6:15**

Course reading: Shiva playing a veena, the two Mahant–Elokeshi scenes and Durga with her family show the range of indigenous popular imagery. Religious subjects and a public social narrative share the market without becoming one story. Distinguish the separate encounter and violence scenes when reading the paired scandal images.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Any recumbent male figure is securely identifiable as Shiva.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: A recumbent male figure or a trident does not, by itself, authenticate every identification as Shiva.
