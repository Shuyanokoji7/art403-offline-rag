# Durga and the buffalo-demon narrative

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Durga’s many weapons and the buffalo or emerging human demon identify a specific narrative of divine victory. A documented museum sculpture shows weapons associated with several gods. Regional and medium-specific versions differ, so do not transfer every weapon count from that sculpture to the lecture print.

## Slide Context

**M6:16**

Course reading: Shiva playing a veena, the two Mahant–Elokeshi scenes and Durga with her family show the range of indigenous popular imagery. Religious subjects and a public social narrative share the market without becoming one story. Distinguish the separate encounter and violence scenes when reading the paired scandal images.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Durga defeating Mahisha and Kali standing over Shiva are the same narrative.”

**Why it is wrong:** Durga defeating Mahisha is not interchangeable with Kali standing over Shiva; identify the opponent and attributes.
