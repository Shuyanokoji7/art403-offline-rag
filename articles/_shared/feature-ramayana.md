# Ramayana imagery across cloth and painting

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: The lecture explicitly identifies Ramayana subject matter and shows Rama, Sita and attendant figures within framed textile narratives. The central enthroned group and surrounding registers are observable organizing devices. This research pass did not establish every small episode or figure in the textile. Those remain unnamed.

## Slide Context

**M2:12**

Course reading: The Golconda coverlet’s pavilion, figures, animals and plants are followed by the Ramayana textile’s framed narrative. This sequence trains close looking before comparing courtly and devotional uses of the same broad textile practice. Shared technique leaves room for different subjects and ways of organizing a field.

**M8:8**

Course reading: The Mumbai trade-textile graphic, ROM display and Southeast Asian Ramayana textile connect contemporary curatorial framing with different uses of Indian cloth. Their sequence distinguishes an exhibition identity, an arranged group of garments and an individual ceremonial textile. Market, subject and present display are different parts of its social history.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Every Ramayana textile uses the same workshop, narrative layout and destination.”

**Why it is wrong:** A Ramayana subject does not dictate one medium, workshop or arrangement; do not give every small panel a guessed episode title.
