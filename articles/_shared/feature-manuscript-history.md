# Manuscript illustration and the history of a page

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Mughal illustrated manuscripts were produced through courtly workshop practices. Narrative text, painting, later borders and album reuse need separate attention. A modern slide or museum image can show only one stage of a page’s history.

## Slide Context

**M5:11**

Course reading: The Christian-imagery canteen and Birth of the Prophet Muhammad are grouped under a discussion of early modern empires and Christianity. Their different media and earlier dates widen the history of Abrahamic imagery beyond one Mughal moment. A thematic heading is not the manufacture date of either object.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The literary text, its illustration and the album mounting must share a single date.”

**Why it is wrong:** Date of the literary text, date of illustration and date of later mounting need not coincide.
