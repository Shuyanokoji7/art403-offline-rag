# Ghalamzani / qalamzani in metalwork

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Qalamzani names traditions of working metal with engraving and related embossing processes. The lecture uses decorated Iranian vessels to discuss such surface work. A tool-related name shared with another craft does not make the processes materially identical.

## Slide Context

**M3:6**

Course reading: The Bidri sailabchi and Iranian ghalamzani vessel compare decorative metalworking across Deccan and Iran. Contrasting inlay on a dark ground and worked surface detail direct attention to different processes. This object-making slide extends the architectural comparison into portable craft.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Qalamzani metalwork uses the same dye process as kalamkari cotton.”

**Why it is wrong:** Qalamzani metalwork and kalamkari textile work act on different supports and use different procedures.
