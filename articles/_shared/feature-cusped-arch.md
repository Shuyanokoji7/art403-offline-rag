# Cusped and foliated arches

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: A cusped arch has repeated lobes along its intrados or opening profile. The Hampi examples use such profiles within larger combinations of architectural forms. Identify the shape separately from the engineering: an ornamental arch can frame a load-bearing opening without itself revealing the complete construction.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “A lobed opening establishes the complete load-bearing technique of the building.”

**Why it is wrong:** A lobed outline establishes an ornament profile, not automatically the material or load-bearing technique.
