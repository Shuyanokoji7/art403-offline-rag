# Botanical illustration and maker credit

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: The Edinburgh account records that Roxburgh commissioned drawings from Indian artists. Scientific authority, collecting, authorship of a botanical text and execution of its images are distinct roles. The corpus therefore retains named commissioners without automatically making them the painters.

## Slide Context

**M6:12**

Course reading: Solvyns’s potters and bazaar, the Kanaree cave view and Roxburgh’s botanical plate form a sequence of people, places and plants under colonial documentation. Each image organizes knowledge differently. The grouping asks who observes, draws, commissions and publishes, as well as what is represented.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Roxburgh’s association with the botanical plates makes him their sole image maker.”

**Why it is wrong:** The RBGE account separates Roxburgh’s commissioning role from the Indian artists who made botanical drawings. Commissioner and image maker are different contributions to the plate’s production.
