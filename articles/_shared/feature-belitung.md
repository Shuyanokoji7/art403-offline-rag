# Belitung shipwreck and maritime cargo

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: The Smithsonian exhibition introduces a cargo dominated by Chinese ceramics, documenting large-scale maritime exchange. A dated bowl helps contextualize the cargo but should not be treated as the exact date of every vessel or the sinking. Manufacture and destination are separate historical questions.

## Slide Context

**M8:9, M8:10, M8:11, M8:12**

Course reading: Belitung bowls arranged as waves around a dhow are compared with underwater documentation and Kerkar’s encrusted cross. The museum uses repeated cargo to give production and shipping a spatial form; the underwater image records another stage of the objects’ lives. The cross introduces the ocean’s material participation in contemporary making.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The dated bowl establishes the exact manufacture and sinking date of every cargo object.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: One dated object cannot independently date every cargo item, and a shipwreck is not evidence that all trade followed one route.
