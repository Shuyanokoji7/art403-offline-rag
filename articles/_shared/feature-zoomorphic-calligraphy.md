# Zoomorphic calligraphy

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: A documented peacock-shaped composition demonstrates writing arranged into an animal form. It provides a parallel for the lecture’s horse-shaped calligraphy, not a reading of that different text. Follow the silhouette and text placement separately from identifying the words.

## Slide Context

**M6:17**

Course reading: The lithographed book cover, tiger-shaped script and hawk-shaped script compare writing as readable content and constructed image. Lithography and colour lithography provide different printed realizations. The grouping connects text, animal outline and circulation rather than treating the animal forms as natural-history studies.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The tiger-shaped composition is a naturalistic wildlife drawing without textual content.”

**Why it is wrong:** The tiger’s body, limbs and tail are built from calligraphic strokes; the paired hawk also combines writing with an animal silhouette. Textual form is the means of making the animal image.
