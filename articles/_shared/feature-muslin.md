# Muslin: fine cotton cloth

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Fine, translucent cotton muslin circulated from India into European dress and was subsequently imitated. Its delicacy is a property of the fabric and weaving, not a floral printing process.

## Attribution Note

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Muslin is a silk printing technique that produces transparent floral patterns.”

**Why it is wrong:** Muslin is a fine cotton fabric. The revived sari and transparency demonstration concern fine woven cloth and its handling; silk, printed flowers and transparency describe different material or visual properties.
