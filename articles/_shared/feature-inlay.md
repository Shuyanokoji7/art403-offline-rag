# Inlay versus incision

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Inlay inserts a contrasting material into a prepared ground. Bidri provides a documented metalwork example in this course. A visible colour contrast is a starting observation. Close inspection or a catalogue is needed to distinguish inserted metal from applied paint or shallow engraving.

## Slide Context

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Inlay is another name for cutting lines without inserting a contrasting material.”

**Why it is wrong:** Inlay adds a material to prepared recesses; engraving cuts a surface without necessarily inserting another material.
