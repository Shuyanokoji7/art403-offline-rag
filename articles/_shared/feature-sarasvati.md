# Sarasvati and Bijapuri musical devotion

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: The Walters record connects Sarasvati with the musical devotion of Ibrahim Adil Shah II. This supports a religious and courtly context for comparison. It is not the catalogue of the slide’s enthroned painting and therefore cannot supply that painting’s date, ownership or exact commission.

## Slide Context

**M3:9**

Course reading: Ibrahim Adil Shah on Atash Khan is paired with enthroned Sarasvati under cultural amalgamation. Courtly self-presentation and a deity of learning and music share the discussion. The next Kitab-e-Nauras page and Sangeet Mahal translate this relationship into text, patronage and a musical setting.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Sarasvati’s appearance in a Bijapuri court setting proves the patron’s religious conversion.”

**Why it is wrong:** A Hindu goddess in a Deccani courtly setting does not prove the patron’s conversion or erase the specificity of the devotional subject.
