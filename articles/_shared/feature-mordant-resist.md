# Mordant and resist dyeing

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: A mordant helps selected dyes bind to cloth. A resist prevents colour reaching protected areas. Both can participate in a multistage textile process. The distinction concerns how colour is fixed or excluded, not simply whether the finished cloth has red and blue areas.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Mordant and resist name the same action of fixing dye to cloth.”

**Why it is wrong:** A mordant helps dye bind to selected areas. A resist protects areas from colour. These complementary operations fix colour and exclude it, respectively.
