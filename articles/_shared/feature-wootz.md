# Wootz steel and material circulation

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Wootz is associated with South Asian crucible-steel production and international circulation of steel and blades. This establishes a material-trade context for the lecture, not a metallurgical identification of a weapon shown only in a painting or relief.

## Slide Context

**M1:16**

Course reading: The trader with horses is placed beside a wootz dagger under Trade connections, with textiles named in the lecture discussion. The grouping connects traded animals, metal and cloth to royal imagery. The collar and performer comparisons on slides 17–18 extend commerce into the movement of dress and visual forms.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “A sword depicted in a relief supplies enough evidence to authenticate wootz metallurgy.”

**Why it is wrong:** A depicted sword or patterned blade is not sufficient evidence to authenticate wootz.
