# Whispering gallery at Gol Gumbaz

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: The district account identifies a whispering gallery associated with Gol Gumbaz. This is an acoustic property of an architectural space, not a separate monument. The source’s inconsistent numerical echo claims are not adopted here.

## Slide Context

**M3:16**

Course reading: Gol Gumbaz’s exterior and structural diagram are followed by a view of another domed complex. The sequence compares the visual massing and setting of Deccani funerary architecture. Keep the uncaptioned complex separate from the named Gol Gumbaz and its documented internal arrangement.
