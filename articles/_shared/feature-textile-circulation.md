# Manufacture, use and collection of textiles

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Indian fabrics moved within established Asian and Indian Ocean markets before European companies entered them. Track production place, intended customer, actual use and present collection as distinct stages.

## Attribution Note

## Slide Context

**M8:8**

Course reading: The Mumbai trade-textile graphic, ROM display and Southeast Asian Ramayana textile connect contemporary curatorial framing with different uses of Indian cloth. Their sequence distinguishes an exhibition identity, an arranged group of garments and an individual ceremonial textile. Market, subject and present display are different parts of its social history.

**Exam distractor:** “Collected in Europe identifies where an Indian textile was manufactured.”

**Why it is wrong:** Made in India and collected in Europe describe different parts of an object history and are compatible.
