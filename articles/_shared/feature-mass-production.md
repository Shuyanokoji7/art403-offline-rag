# Reproducibility and production scale

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: The Belitung cargo documents large-scale ceramic output, while print technology enables repeated impressions from prepared matrices. These are different manufacturing systems.

## Slide Context

**M8:9, M8:10, M8:12**

Course reading: Belitung bowls arranged as waves around a dhow are compared with underwater documentation and Kerkar’s encrusted cross. The museum uses repeated cargo to give production and shipping a spatial form; the underwater image records another stage of the objects’ lives. The cross introduces the ocean’s material participation in contemporary making.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Repetition proves industrial machinery and mechanically identical production.”

**Why it is wrong:** Reproducible, mass-produced and mechanically identical are not interchangeable descriptions.
