# Lion and lamb in imperial allegory

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: In the Jahangir–Shah Abbas comparison, the animals and unequal rulers contribute to an idealized, hierarchical order. Scholarship situates the globe and privileged imperial position within claims of sovereignty. The peaceful pairing does not make the rulers equal in the composition.

## Slide Context

**M5:21**

Course reading: Jahangir’s embrace of Shah Abbas uses a mapped globe and unequal figure scale for an allegory of sovereignty; the next folio places Jahangir with Jesus in separate images. The sequence compares the court’s political and religious visual resources. An embrace or shared page does not establish equal power or a single religious identity.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The embrace, lion and lamb show that Jahangir and Shah Abbas hold equal power in the painting.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: Jahangir is represented at the larger scale within an allegory of sovereignty. The embrace and animal pairing serve that political image; they do not cancel the hierarchy created by the two rulers’ unequal sizes.
