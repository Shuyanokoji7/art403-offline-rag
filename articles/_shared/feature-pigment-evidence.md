# Visible colour versus identified pigment

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Colour, gilding and material names should be separated. A technical study can combine visual examination with material evidence to investigate a painting’s manufacture. In this corpus, visible blue or gold-coloured areas remain colour descriptions unless a caption or specific source identifies the material.

## Slide Context

**M5:14**

Course reading: The two Camel Fight images by or associated with Bihzad and Abd al-Samad are paired under Lineage or Silsila. A recurring subject and composition make transmission and emulation visible. The succeeding European-source studies extend this enquiry beyond one Persianate line of artistic association.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Blue and gold-coloured areas prove lapis lazuli and gold leaf.”

**Why it is wrong:** Blue does not establish lapis lazuli, and gold colour does not establish gold leaf or an overseas supply route.
