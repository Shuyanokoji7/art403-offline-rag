# Entrance towers and sanctuary superstructures

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: The Hampi material differentiates prominent temple entrance towers from superstructures over other parts of a complex. Position and function must be read with form. A pyramidal outline reused on a courtly pavilion does not transform that pavilion into a temple gate.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Every pyramidal temple roof is an entrance gopuram.”

**Why it is wrong:** A gopuram marks a temple entrance; a shikhara rises over a sanctuary. The position and function of the tower distinguish the terms, even when both have tapering profiles.
