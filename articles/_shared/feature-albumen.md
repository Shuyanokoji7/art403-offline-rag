# Albumen photographs and applied colour

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: The Beato exhibition record identifies hand-coloured albumen photographs. The photographic image and later colouring are separate operations, potentially involving different makers. This supports attention to the mediation of the represented scene.

## Slide Context

**M7:13**

Course reading: The photographic portraits of Monet and Cassatt are followed by Beato’s staged performers. These photographs connect the artists and Japan to specific modes of representation. Sitter identification, photographic authorship, staging and hand-colouring are separate questions rather than one transparent documentary fact.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Beato’s hand-coloured albumen image was captured directly in colour like a modern colour photograph.”

**Why it is wrong:** A hand-coloured photograph is not a colour photograph made by a modern colour-sensitive process.
