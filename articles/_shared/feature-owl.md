# The owl in Jahangir’s Malik Ambar allegory

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: A scholarly study connects the owl beside the target with hostile and occult imagery directed against Malik Ambar. The image imagines a triumph that Jahangir had not achieved. The bird’s exact placement belongs to the visual analysis. This interpretation is specific to the documented political context.

## Slide Context

**M5:19, M5:20**

Course reading: The full Jahangir–Malik Ambar allegory and its details connect the emperor’s body to a mapped globe, cosmic supports and a defeated enemy. The India-centred globe is part of a political claim. Repeated enlargement trains attention to the small devices through which cartographic form becomes imperial imagery.
