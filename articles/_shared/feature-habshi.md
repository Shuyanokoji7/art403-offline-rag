# African participation in Deccani courts

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: The V&A account of Malik Ambar supplies a documented example of African political power in India. The lecture separately identifies Khawwas-Khan as an Abyssinian commander. These are distinct individuals.

## Slide Context

**M3:22**

Course reading: Khawwas-Khan’s portrait is framed by Witsen’s collecting activity and Dutch commercial connections. The painted courtier, album annotation and European collector are distinct agents. This follows the city-scale discussion by tracing how a Deccani political image entered an overseas collection.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Any African courtier in a Deccani portrait can be identified as Malik Ambar.”

**Why it is wrong:** Habshi-related context does not make every African courtier Malik Ambar, nor does a European collector become the portrait’s original patron.
