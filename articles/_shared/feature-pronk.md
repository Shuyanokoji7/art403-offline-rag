# Pronk and sumptuous display

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Pronk still life emphasizes sumptuous objects and conspicuous abundance. Reflective vessels, costly foods and imported wares can demonstrate pictorial skill while evoking wealth and circulation. Interpret these effects with the specific arrangement, rather than treating luxury as a complete explanation.

## Slide Context

**M4:5**

Course reading: The VOC ship model and lavish lobster-and-fruit still life connect transport with the consumption and display of goods. Between Amsterdam and Asia develops into a discussion of materiality and trade. The painted table is therefore read through economic circulation as well as arrangement, colour and texture.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Every still life is a pronk display, regardless of the selected objects.”

**Why it is wrong:** Pronk describes ostentatious display; it is not a synonym for every still life or a specific pigment technique.
