# Dome and monumental enclosure

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: At Gol Gumbaz the dome establishes the dominant mass above the tomb’s large enclosed volume. Exterior silhouette, the supporting arrangement shown in a diagram and interior acoustics provide different kinds of evidence about the same structure.

## Slide Context

**M3:16**

Course reading: Gol Gumbaz’s exterior and structural diagram are followed by a view of another domed complex. The sequence compares the visual massing and setting of Deccani funerary architecture. Keep the uncaptioned complex separate from the named Gol Gumbaz and its documented internal arrangement.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Gol Gumbaz’s dome and corner towers are interchangeable structural elements.”

**Why it is wrong:** A dome is not the same element as a corner tower or minaret; distinguish spanning volume from vertical accent.
