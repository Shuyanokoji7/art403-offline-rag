# Chiaroscuro: light and dark modelling

## Definition and Grounded Significance

Course reading: Chiaroscuro concerns the organization of light and dark to establish form and pictorial effect. The National Gallery’s Leonardo discussion provides a specific context. The term describes tonal handling rather than an identifiable chemical pigment or a guarantee of documentary realism.

## Slide Context

**M8:13**

Course reading: The reclining beach figure, Caravaggio’s Sleeping Cupid and the painting’s placement above paper boats connect bodily vulnerability with a Mediterranean exhibition setting. Height and floor placement make the comparison specific. The paper boats belong to the display, and the beach figure remains a separate work rather than a view of Caravaggio’s painted figure.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Chiaroscuro and sfumato are interchangeable names for a dark background.”

**Why it is wrong:** Chiaroscuro concerns tonal contrast; sfumato concerns softened transitions and contours.
