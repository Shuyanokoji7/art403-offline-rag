# A Goldsmith in his Shop

## External Checks

- **Qualification:** The Met treats the mirror’s moral reading as a possibility and explains that a later-added halo was removed. The shopkeeper should not therefore be asserted as Saint Eligius on the basis of that halo. [Petrus Christus, A Goldsmith in his Shop — The Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/459052)

## Composition

A seated red-clad goldsmith occupies the foreground, with a richly dressed couple standing behind. Shelves of goods crowd the upper right. A broad counter closes the bottom.

## Perspective Convention

The room encloses the figures, while the convex reflection inserts another spatial field within it.

## Figural Style

The goldsmith holds a small balance. The woman extends a hand while the man stands behind her, his hand at her shoulder.

## Text–Image Relationship

A painted signature/date strip runs along the counter’s front edge.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

The convex mirror at lower right reflects a street and two people. Coins and weights sit beside the scales. Coral-like branches, jewellery, vessels and a bead strand fill the shelving. A long red girdle curls over the left counter edge.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The convex mirror shows an empty reflection and contributes nothing beyond the central weighing scene.”

**Why it is wrong:** The lower-right convex mirror reflects a street and two people. It introduces an exterior view within the shop scene rather than repeating an empty surface.
