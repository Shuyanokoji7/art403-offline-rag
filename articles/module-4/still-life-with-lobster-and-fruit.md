# Still Life with Lobster and Fruit

## Composition

Objects rise diagonally from the bright red lobster at lower left to a tall elaborate vessel at upper centre. Fruit spills around a decorated bowl at right.

## Perspective Convention

Foreshortened dishes and overlapping objects share a shallow tabletop stage.

## Palette & Pigment

Red shell and yellow peel punctuate the brown-dark ground. Concentrated pale highlights distinguish glass, metal, rind and wet fruit.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

A partly peeled citrus fruit trails its skin over the table edge. Grapes surround a cut melon at right. A large metal dish leans at left behind the lobster. Translucent drinking vessels overlap the metallic ware.
