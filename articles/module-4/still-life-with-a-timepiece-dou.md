# Still Life with a Timepiece — Dou

## Composition

An arched niche frames a suspended watch at left and a tall candle at right. A half-drawn curtain hangs from rings across the upper opening.

## Palette & Pigment

Small pale and metallic highlights emerge from a predominantly dark brown-green niche.

## Line & Hand

The watch face, curtain rings, book edges and folds receive close definition.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

A blue ribbon suspends the watch. A long pipe runs diagonally over a printed sheet. A brass-coloured candlestick rests on a thick book with loose ribbons. The candle is unlit in the image.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Dou’s timepiece establishes the exact time when the still life was painted.”

**Why it is wrong:** The timepiece is a represented crafted object, not a timestamp for making the painting. Course reading: it joins an attention to time with the labour invested in objects and their depiction.
