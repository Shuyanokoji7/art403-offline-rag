# Ocean at the Table — commodities and still life

## Argument

Course reading: The module treats still life as an entry into maritime trade, material knowledge, wealth and artisanal labour. Its 1609–1713 Dutch Golden Age frame links overseas commerce with changes in Dutch society.

## Evidence & Artworks

Course reading: Vosmaer’s flowers, Vermeer’s maps and carpet, van Beyeren’s luxurious food, Dou’s manufactured objects, van Aelst’s armour and d’Hondecoeter’s animals provide different forms of evidence.

## Course Throughline

Course reading: Objects and organisms circulate with people and knowledge. A still life can therefore be studied beyond genre classification, while keeping the pictured object separate from the commodity’s documented origin.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “An oceanic reading requires every object in every still life to be an imported commodity.”

**Why it is wrong:** Course reading: A trade-based reading does not require every painted object to be imported.
