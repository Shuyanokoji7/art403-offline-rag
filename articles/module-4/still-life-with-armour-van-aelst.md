# Still Life with Armour — van Aelst

## Composition

Shining armour and vessels form a descending diagonal from the upper-right shield to the helmet and shoe-like armour at lower left. White cloth bridges table and chair.

## Perspective Convention

Oblique furniture edges and stacked objects establish recession through a staged interior.

## Palette & Pigment

Silver-grey reflections and gold-coloured ornament contrast with red upholstery and deep shadow.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

A breastplate leans near the shield. A sword lies across the red-upholstered chair. A hanging dark curtain frames the top. Embossed vessels occupy the rear-left tabletop.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Van Aelst’s armour still life documents an actual battle in progress.”

**Why it is wrong:** The armour is arranged as an object within a still life. Its represented surfaces and surrounding possessions establish a display, rather than the action of a battle.
