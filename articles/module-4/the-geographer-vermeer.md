# The Geographer — Vermeer

## Composition

A man bends over a table at centre, framed by the bright window at left and a cupboard topped by a globe behind. A large patterned textile fills the foreground.

## Perspective Convention

Window, cabinet and table establish a coherent domestic interior. Overlapping cloth and papers create the foreground depth.

## Palette & Pigment

Blue robe, warm yellow light and brown furnishings organize the room.

## Figural Style

The blue-robed figure holds dividers above a chart and looks toward the window rather than at the viewer.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

A globe sits high on the cupboard. A framed wall map hangs at right. Loose rolls and sheets lie on the floor at lower right. The table carpet is deeply folded.
