# A Vase with Flowers — Vosmaer

## Composition

A tall bouquet fills almost the whole panel above a metal-coloured handled vase. Blossoms turn in different directions, while isolated fallen petals and flowers rest on the stone ledge below.

## Perspective Convention

Overlapping blossoms create depth within a narrow frontal space. The vessel and ledge anchor the bouquet.

## Palette & Pigment

Striped yellow-red tulips, pink blossoms and a blue-white iris stand out against near-black foliage. These are colour observations.

## Line & Hand

Sharp petal edges and individually differentiated flower surfaces produce the naturalistic effect.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

A pale butterfly appears low in the bouquet near the central striped tulip. Drooping stems and detached flowers interrupt the apparent plenitude.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “Vosmaer’s bouquet records one naturally occurring group of flowers exactly as gathered.”

**Why it is wrong:** This is a painting of flowers, not a botanical inventory proving that all species bloomed together or were imported by the same route.
