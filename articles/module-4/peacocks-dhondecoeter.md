# Peacocks — d’Hondecoeter

## Composition

A peacock stands high on a stone ledge at right, its long tail sweeping diagonally to the lower left. A pale long-legged bird balances it at left.

## Perspective Convention

Foreground fruit and animals lead back to a garden with trees and distant sky.

## Palette & Pigment

Iridescent-looking blue-green plumage and repeated tail eyes contrast with earthy masonry and foliage.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

A monkey sits beside grapes and a cut melon at lower right. A small reddish furry animal crouches under the tail. A dark bird flies across the upper-left sky. A sunflower rises behind the peacock at upper right. Another bird bends downward from the ledge.

## Slide Context

### Module 4, slide 9

**Heading context:** Trade goods and “Exotic” on the Table and in the Garden

Course reading: Peacocks in a garden and dead partridges in a still life compare living display with arranged game. The trade-goods and exotic discussion asks how birds enter collections, consumption and images. Their shared animal subject does not make the scenes interchangeable or give them one fixed symbolic code.

## Distinctions

**Exam distractor:** “The peacocks and partridges illustrate the same treatment of birds as dead game.”

**Why it is wrong:** The peacocks occupy a living garden scene; the partridges on the following slide are dead game in an arranged still life. The comparison changes the birds’ condition and pictorial use.
