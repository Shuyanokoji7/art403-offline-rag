# Still Life with Partridges — van Aelst

## Composition

Dead game lies diagonally across a green bag on a ledge, with one pale wing spread toward the viewer. Equipment hangs beyond the ledge into the dark foreground.

## Palette & Pigment

White and grey feathers catch the light against brown shadow, green cloth and small red accents.

## Line & Hand

Distinct feather groupings and polished buckles provide the material differentiation.

## Motifs & Hidden Details

A small dark fly sits on the pale wing. Metal buckles, cords, a broad ochre strap and a red-tipped accessory articulate the lower half. A horn-like vessel angles upward at left.
