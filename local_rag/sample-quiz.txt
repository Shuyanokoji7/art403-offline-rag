Which description best fits Toulouse-Lautrec's Jane Avril chromolithograph?

B. A photographic halftone reproduction of a studio portrait
C. Monochrome etching with dense cross-hatching

How did Manet's and Van Gogh's appreciation of Japan differ?

B. Manet painted water lilies while Van Gogh ignored Japanese art
C. Neither artist showed any aesthetic appreciation of Japanese prints
D. Both treated Japanese prints purely as commercial merchandise

What characterises Calcutta chromolithographs?
A. Regional aesthetics, European stories and European print technology
B. Regional narratives combined with regional and European aesthetics
C. Exclusively European subject matter and European techniques
D. Hand-painted miniatures with no printing involved
